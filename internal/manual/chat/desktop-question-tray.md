# Question tray

## Arrow keys in the question tray, You decide on a clarification, Holding up a question

When more than one question is waiting, left and right arrow keys move between questions while the tray is focused. The corner reads `1 of 3`. The arrow at the end is disabled and drawn at 40% opacity. Arrows typed in the message field do not change the page.

A single permission shows `Allow once`, `Deny`, and `Always allow…`. Enter chooses Allow once. Always allow… asks how far the yes reaches. Something that cannot be undone has no clock, no Always, and no reason field. `Keep it` is first and quiet. `Remove` is the soft danger answer. `Tell it…` waits for words before it sends.

Typing in the card holds the countdown. The line then reads `On hold — take your time`.

On a clarification, Command Enter (⌘↵, or Ctrl Enter) sends what you typed. `Later` folds that question into the header count, such as `1 later`. `You decide` answers with the asker's own suggestion. Both are the same quiet ink. You decide is absent when the question has no suggestion.

`One by one` on a batch of permissions turns that card into a pager. `Deny all` declines every command.

When a question blocks tasks, the foot reads `Holding up 2 tasks: Update fixtures, Port fix to v1`.

Click a receipt to open that question, or press Enter on it, and that question comes forward. A clock pick reads `picked by codeaf after 30s`. An answer you gave reads like `Use Postgres · you · 14:02`. A question the turn left behind reads `No longer needed. The turn moved on.`

The scrolling middle stops at 40% of the window height. When more text sits below, a 16px fade covers the bottom. The answer buttons stay put. The card rises with the 380ms spring.
