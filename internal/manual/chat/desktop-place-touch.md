# Opening a place counts as touching it

## Does going to a place count as touching it?

Going to a place counts as touching it. Choosing it in Go to (⌘P, or Ctrl P), clicking it on the rail, or opening it in a new window records the visit. The line that says “Name” hasn’t been touched in N days then moves on to the next place that is still due.

Now and All places are not visits. A visit the engine refuses still opens the place, and that line stays until a later visit succeeds. The visit is not something you can undo, and it does not rename the place or change its chats or files.
