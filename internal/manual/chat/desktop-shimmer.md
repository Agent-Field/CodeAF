# Shimmer and the breathing dot

## Why is the live step shimmering on the desktop?

A live step's title is the only line that shimmers. A bright band of ordinary ink moves across the quieter ink and then repeats. One pass takes 2.4 seconds. The words stay those two inks; they do not turn amber, red or the accent. When the step settles, the sweep stops and the title keeps whatever colour that row uses. Nothing else on the desktop is supposed to shimmer.

## What is the breathing dot next to N running?

The conversation header is the only place a dot breathes. Next to the running count, such as "4 running", sits a 6px accent dot. Its soft halo eases out and back over 2.8 seconds. The words are the name; the dot itself is not announced. Rows, tabs and every other status mark stay still. Elapsed time is what shows that other work is moving.

## How do I stop the desktop shimmer and the breathing dot?

Turn on reduced motion in the system settings. The live title then sits still in the quieter ink, and the header dot sits still with no halo. There is no separate switch inside codeaf. The clock on a running step still advances.
