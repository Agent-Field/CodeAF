# Desktop place rail sections

## What is on the desktop place rail?

The desktop place rail lists, in order: Now, then Pinned, then Open, then All places at the bottom.

Pinned and Open headings appear only when that section has a place. Archived places are never listed. There is no Inbox row. Questions in other conversations are the frame pill, not a rail row.

With places in the graph but none pinned or open, the rail says: Places you open show here. Pin the ones you live in.

With no places yet, the rail shows Now and All places only. There is no heading and no shortcut beside All places. Once a place exists, All places shows its shortcut (⌘⇧P on macOS, Ctrl Shift P elsewhere).

Open has Close all on the right of its heading. That closes the Open places in this window. It does not delete them.

## How do I move between rows on the desktop place rail?

Arrow up and arrow down move through Now, the place rows and All places. The move stops at the first and last row. Enter activates the focused row: Now, Go to that place, or All places.

The focused row shows a two-pixel accent ring and a four-pixel halo, and only after a keyboard focus. A click selects without that ring.

Command-click (Control-click on Linux) or middle-click on a place opens it in a new window. The same gesture on Now opens a new window on Now. On All places it opens a new tab.

## Where is the desktop inbox row?

There is no Inbox row on the desktop place rail, and no Inbox tab. Anything that needs you in another conversation is counted by the frame pill. The current conversation keeps its own count.

## What is the number next to Now on the desktop place rail?

The number is how many chats are not in any place. Zero shows nothing, not 0. A screen reader hears it as a description of the Now row, whose name stays Now.

An amber dot on Now means something there needs you. A red dot means a task failed. Running work alone draws no dot.

## How do I close every open place on the desktop rail?

Hover the Open heading, or focus it, and press Close all. On a touch screen Close all stays visible. Pinned places are not closed by it. A place that is still running or needs you can remain, muted, until that work settles.
