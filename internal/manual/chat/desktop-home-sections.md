# Desktop place Home

## What is on my place Home and what order is it in?

A populated desktop place Home starts with its breadcrumb and name menu. Below the name, the engine's status says whether the place is learning, deciding automatically, or always asking you. Unknown status shows nothing.

An engine-written Since recap comes next when available, followed by Live work, Decided automatically, and What the place knows. Live includes work whose tab was closed. Decided automatically opens the recorded reason when you select a row. What the place knows shows real saved lines and where they came from.

The knowledge field sits below those lines; the Start something composer sits at the bottom. All places keeps its search, place tiles and chats in no place. An empty place keeps its existing sentence and Add files or links and Write instructions actions.

## Where are learning proposals on Home?

Learning has a status line on Home, not a proposals list. A proposal belongs with its question in the conversation tray or Next up. Past automatic decisions are separate from proposals.

## Add something this place should know from Home

Type in “Add something [place] should know” and press Enter. The line appears after the engine saves it. A failure keeps your words; a conflicting line reports the conflict without saving a replacement. Saved lines can be edited or removed, and the change offers Undo. When the engine is unreachable, changes are paused. A section read failure offers Retry Home sections while keeping other available sections visible.
