# Desktop home tab

## Where is the pinned home tab in the desktop strip

There is no pinned home tab any more. A place's Home is an ordinary tab in the strip: it opens right after the tab you are on when you click the place in the rail, and it focuses the one already open instead of opening another. It shows the place's colour square and the place's name. It has a close mark like any other tab, and it can be moved, grouped and split. Closing it closes only that tab.

## How do I open the place switcher from the home tab

The home tab is not a place switcher any more. With the rail hidden, press Command+P (Control+P on Linux) for Go to, or use the Control (Alt on Linux) number keys. Going to a place opens its Home as a tab.

## Why is there an amber mark on the home tab swatch

A Home tab shows the same marks as other tabs: its colour square carries the tab's own state, and the rail row carries a place that needs you. The home tab no longer announces another place when the rail is hidden.

## How do I open the place menu on the home tab

Right-click a Home tab for the ordinary tab menu. The place menu is on the place's rail row and on the Home page itself.
