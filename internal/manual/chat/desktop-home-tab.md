# Desktop home tab

## Where is the pinned home tab in the desktop strip

The first tab in a place's strip is that place's home tab. It shows the place's colour square and the place's name, and it is shorter than the other tabs. The tab you are in sits on the page colour with a slight lift. The others are flat. A thin vertical line sits just after the home tab, before the rest of the strip. There is no close mark on it. Closing the tab you are working in does not close home.

## How do I open the place switcher from the home tab

Hide the rail. The home tab then shows an up-down chevron after the place name. Click that tab and the place switcher opens. With the rail showing, a click on the home tab goes to that place's home instead, and the chevron is not there.

## Why is there an amber mark on the home tab swatch

The colour square picks up a small amber dot when another place needs you and the rail is hidden, so the need is still visible. The tab says which place. The words stay the ordinary colour. A blank notice draws no dot. While the rail is open the rail row carries that news, and the home tab's square stays plain.

## How do I open the place menu on the home tab

Right-click the home tab. That opens the place menu, the same menu as a right-click on the place. Keyboard focus draws a ring around the tab. A click with the pointer does not.
