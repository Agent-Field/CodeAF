# Desktop Why? popover

## Why did the place decide that — what the Why? card on a receipt shows

An automatic decision leaves a receipt line that ends in “Why?”. Pressing it opens a
small card titled “Decided automatically” with up to three facts: By (the place that
decided), Because (the reason, for example “You allowed go test here 6 times.
Reversible.”) and Sure (how confident the place was, as a percentage). A fact the
engine did not record is left out of the card rather than shown as a blank or “0%”.

## Overturn a decision, or make a place always ask me — Next time on the Why? card

Under the facts, “Next time…” offers two exclusive choices: “Always ask me”, which
sends this kind of question to you from now on, and “Fine, keep deciding”, which
leaves the place deciding it. Neither is selected until you choose. “Overturn” undoes
the decision and drops that kind of question back to learning. “Open the decision”
takes you to where it was made. Overturn and Open the decision close the card.

## Closing the Why? card — Escape and focus

Escape, or a press anywhere outside the card, closes it. Escape also puts keyboard
focus back on “Why?” so you can reopen it. The card fades in from a 98% scale at its
top-left corner, and appears without motion when reduced motion is on.
