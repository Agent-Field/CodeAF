# Overview card menu

## How do I right-click an overview card?

In the desktop tab overview, right-click a card, or focus the card and press Shift+F10. That menu is the tab's own menu, in the same order: Open in split, Add to group, Pin tab, Duplicate, Rename tab, then Copy link when the tab has a conversation to link to, then Close tab. Close and stop is listed only while the work is still going. Then Close other tabs and Close tabs to the right. Move to new window is listed only in the desktop app, where a tab can actually move. Arrow keys move through the menu, Enter chooses, and Escape closes it and puts focus back on the card.

## Why does a terminal card in the overview show no lines?

A terminal card in the tab overview shows its last output lines only when that tab points at a terminal. A diff card shows the first changed lines only when that tab points at a file. With no target, the card shows the title and no lines. codeaf does not invent output.

## What does an empty conversation card show in the overview?

An empty conversation card in the tab overview shows the title and the word Conversation. With no draft and no reply, it adds no sentence about the conversation being empty.
