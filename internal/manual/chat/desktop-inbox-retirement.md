# Desktop Inbox retirement

## What happens to my saved Inbox tab after updating the desktop app

The desktop no longer has an Inbox tab or a pinned Inbox slot. Updating drops those saved slots without deleting your conversations. Other open tabs, their drafts, groups, and closed chats remain available. A saved workspace containing only Inbox opens one quiet New tab; it does not start a conversation.

## What happens to an old desktop Inbox link

An old `codeaf://inbox` link starts Next up. It does not recreate Inbox or start a conversation. Next up uses the questions already supplied by the engine and opens them in their own conversations. It draws no invented question when there is nothing waiting.

## Does moving my workspace to a place also move the old Inbox

No. Workspace transfer offers ignore retired Inbox slots. Your saved chats and their drafts move through the ordinary workspace transfer; Inbox does not become a chat.
