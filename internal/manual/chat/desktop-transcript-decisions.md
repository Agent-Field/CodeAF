# Desktop transcript decisions

## Why does the desktop show Allowed automatically by a place?

An automatic decision leaves its recorded decision line in the conversation. The place and reason come from the saved engine entry, so reopening the conversation redraws the decision line. Why? reveals the recorded place, reason and confidence when known. This transcript integration currently shows the explanation as a quiet line; overturn and next-time controls are not wired here.

## What does Did 2 things mean in the desktop transcript?

A grouped decision line opens to its individual decisions. Use its chevron or sentence to expand it. Remembered notes stay in conversation order. Undo appears only when the engine supplied exact undo tokens and the Places connection is available.

## Where are Go Edit Cancel on a desktop plan proposal?

A structured plan aside renders Go, Edit and Cancel. Go sends the plan to the engine; edits are submitted before Go. Cancel dismisses the plan only after the engine accepts cancellation. A refused request keeps the proposal available and displays the engine's reason. The current engine sends plans as live events rather than saved plan asides; this transcript mapping cannot restore a plan from a journal that never recorded it.

## Why does the desktop tray say a place is learning?

The learning mark supplies the place and agreement count. With a proposed option and another choice, the tray shows Agree and Choose. Agree sends the proposed key; Choose records an overrule. While scrolled away, the same pending item remains reachable from the compact Review line.
