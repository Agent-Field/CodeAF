# The proposal card in the question tray

## What is the card that says a place is learning

While a place is still learning a kind of question, the question tray does not ask it cold. The tray's top line reads "Marketing is learning · 14 of 20 agreed" with the amber mark, and the card under it is the usual question with the place's own answer already selected. The proposed choice is lit and carries a second line, "Would choose · 92% · matches what Marketing knows". The other choice sits unlit beside it. Nothing is added to the Home page; the proposal only ever appears in the tray.

## How do I agree with a proposal

Press Agree, or press Enter anywhere in the card that is not a button. Agree sends the proposed choice exactly, and it counts as an agreement toward the 20 answers the place needs. It is one click.

## How do I take the other answer instead of the proposal

Press the second button, "Choose" followed by the other choice's name. Picking a different choice in the card first changes which name the button carries. That answer is sent as overruled: it counts against the proposal, not toward graduating, and it is recorded so the place learns from it.

## What does the proposal say it relied on

Hover the proposed choice. When the engine named the knows lines and earlier decisions it weighed, the hover reads "Relied on" followed by their ids. When it named none, there is no hover and no empty "because". If the engine did not measure a percentage, the "92%" part is left out rather than shown as zero.

## Does the proposal countdown stop when I start reading it

No. A proposal with a countdown holds it the moment you focus anything inside the card, as every question card does. A held clock reads "On hold — take your time".
