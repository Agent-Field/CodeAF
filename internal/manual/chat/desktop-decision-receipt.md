# Desktop decision receipts

## Why does it say allowed automatically

When a place decides on its own, the conversation keeps a quiet line where that happened. A 12px sparkle sits in front, in the same quiet ink as the words. The line reads `Allowed automatically by Config parser · you always allow this here · Why?`. The place's name is a step darker than the rest of the line. The words in the middle are the reason the place had. If it sent no reason, that clause is left off and the line still ends with Why?.

## What does Why do on a receipt

Why? is a button at the end of an automatic decision, in the same darker ink as the place's name. Pressing it asks why this was decided. Pressing the rest of the line does not. The explanation is not written on the line itself.

## What happens when I press an allowed automatically line

Pressing the words of an automatic decision, and not Why?, brings that decision forward in the tray. A line with no place name is not drawn.

## What does did 3 things mean

Several actions in a row fold into one line: `Did 3 things · held Launch post, asked Software, saved a note`. The short phrases are a step darker, and a small chevron sits at the end. Pressing the chevron shows each action's own line, a small gap apart. Pressing one of those brings that decision forward in the tray. One action stays a single line. It does not say "Did 1 thing".

## What does a policy denial look like

A permission the place's policy refused shows as the same quiet line. It reads `Not allowed by Security · this place does not allow it · Why?`, with the place's own name and the reason it sent. The ink does not turn red, and nothing pops up a dialog. Pressing the words still brings that decision forward in the tray.
