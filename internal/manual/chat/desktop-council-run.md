# How a discussion between two places runs

## How do two places talk to each other

Each turn one of the two places replies in the council chat, using only what
that place knows. The place that opened the discussion speaks first and the two
take turns. When both agree, a reply begins with "Decided:" and the discussion
ends. The outcome is saved in both places as a knows line that says it was said
in that chat.

## What happens when two places cannot agree

A discussion stops at 6 turns or $0.25, whichever comes first. The turn that
crosses $0.25 is still paid for and is the last one. It is then escalated to the
nearest place that sits above both. When the two places share no parent, it
comes to you as a question that needs you, under capped councils.

## How do I step into a discussion between two places

Start typing in the council chat and the discussion pauses before its next turn.
A turn already being written finishes first. When you send your message the
places carry on and answer you first. Your message does not count as a turn,
but the replies it brings do count toward the $0.25.

## What happens if a place fails to reply

The discussion is left paused with the turns it already has, and goes on from
there when resumed. Nothing is decided or escalated because of a failed reply.
After a restart a resumed discussion carries on without the earlier lines in
front of it, and the places reply from what they know and the topic alone.
