# Place tiles on the desktop

## What does a place tile show on the desktop home

A place on the desktop home is an 84px tile. It shows that place's colour square, its name, and a count when there is one: "28 chats", "6 chats · also in Software", or "47 places · 212 chats". A count of zero is left off. Hover fills the tile. A click opens the place.

## Why is there an amber or red dot on a place tile

A six-pixel amber dot means something in that place, or in a place inside it, needs you. A red dot means a task failed. If both are true, the amber dot is the one shown. Work that is only running does not put a dot on the tile. The name stays the ordinary text colour; only the dot is coloured.

## What does Add here mean on a place tile

While you drag a chat or a place over a tile that can take it, the tile says "Add here". Dropping adds it to that place. Letting go elsewhere leaves things as they were.

## How do I add a new place from the tile

The last tile says "New place". Choosing it turns that tile into a name and a tint. Enter creates the place. Escape puts the New place tile back.

## How do I tell which place tile is selected

The selected tile has a 2px accent ring and no soft halo. Keyboard focus is separate: Tab draws the usual 2px accent ring with a 4px halo, and a click does not.
