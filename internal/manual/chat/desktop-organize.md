# Organize desktop places

## How do I organize my old chats into places

Open Settings, find Places organization, and press "Organize now". codeaf reads your saved chats and offers a plan. Progress reads "Reading", "Grouping", "Naming" or "Checking", with counts only when the engine supplies them. The previous run's summary appears when there is one. An engine without this feature shows no Organize now control.

Organizing finds related conversations, keeps very similar copies together, and sets aside short test conversations. A chat stays in a broader place when it does not clearly fit a more specific one. A chat that fits no place stays in Now. Names come from the conversations themselves.

## What is Add to what's there versus Start fresh

"Add to what's there" is the default. It files chats into the places you already have and may offer new places for related chats in Now. Existing places keep their identity, names and members.

"Start fresh" builds a new arrangement for review. Places you made yourself are protected: their chats stay together, and renaming or removing those places requires your approval. Places created by codeaf may be rearranged. Read the plan's summary to see what would change before accepting.

## How do I review the plan and leave out a change

"Review" shows the engine's summary and individual changes. Every change starts checked; uncheck any you do not want. "Accept" applies the included changes together. "Not now" declines the plan. Closing Review leaves the plan available to review again.

New places, renames, merges and changes to the arrangement ask first. Views can appear without approval because they only filter existing chats. If "File chats without asking" is on, eligible filings into existing places can happen without accepting the plan.

## Can I cancel organizing or undo the accepted plan

Press "Cancel" while organizing. A cancelled run applies nothing and its last-run line reads "Cancelled" when there is no summary. Only one organizing run can happen at a time.

After you accept, "Undo" takes the whole accepted plan back with one action. The Settings control keeps that Undo available during the current app session, including if you leave Settings and return.

## What happens if organizing reaches its spending limit

Places organization has "Organizing calls per run" (default 150) and "Organizing cost per run" (default 5 US cents). Past either budget, organizing continues without further naming or interpretation calls. The resulting plan can be provisional; read the engine's summary before accepting. Missing or unusable model answers use rules and available names instead of inventing an answer. No measured price for your run means no price is shown.
