# Filing a chat into a place

## After the first reply, which place gets the chat

After the first reply, the chat is put in a place that already exists, or it stays in Now. If the chat's folder already belongs to a place, that place is the one, and the reason reads "This chat's folder already belongs to" followed by the place's name. Otherwise the reason reads "This chat fits" followed by the place's name. When a finer place does not fit clearly better than the broader one above it, the chat is kept at the broader place.

## When codeaf asks which place a chat belongs in

codeaf asks only when a chat fits two places in different parts of the library about equally. It shows up to five existing places by name, and "none of these". There is no choice, in that question, to begin a place. Picking one of the places puts the chat there. Picking "none of these" leaves the chat in Now. If the question is not answered, the chat is kept at the broader of the two places that were close.

## Why a chat stays in Now instead of a place

A chat stays in Now when no place fits it. The reason reads "Nothing open fits this chat, so it stays in Now". It also stays in Now when the answer to the place question is "none of these". A chat that does not fit is not an offer of a place.

## When Now offers a new place

Now offers a new place when at least four chats sitting in Now belong together. The offer says "These chats in Now belong together". The place has no name until it is named, and nothing is created until the offer is accepted. Three chats that belong together are not enough, and a chat that is a check or a copy of another chat does not count and cannot join two groups into one. A second group that also has four or more is a separate offer.

## A single arriving chat does not begin a place

One arriving chat is put into a place, kept at a broader place, or left in Now. It does not begin a place by arriving. A place is offered only from chats already in Now, and only once four of them belong together.

## While a draft is still being typed, which place would it fit

While a draft is still being typed, codeaf can name the place it would fit, and it writes nothing. A folder that already belongs to a place is the answer. Otherwise it names the closest place that fits, with a small tip toward a place you were in during the last hour. Up to two other places that also fit can be named with it. When nothing fits, it names no place. It does not ask a question, and the chat is not put anywhere until it is filed.
