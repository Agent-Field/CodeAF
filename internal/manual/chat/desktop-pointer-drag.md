# Dragging with the pointer

## How do I drag a tab to reorder it

Press a tab and move it. The first 4px is still a click, so a short press selects the tab. Past that, the tab lifts and follows the pointer. Drop it on the left or right quarter of another tab to put it before or after that tab. Middle-click and right-click never start a drag. A pinned tab reorders the same way and does not group.

## How do I drag a tab onto another to group them

Drop the tab on the middle of another tab. That middle says "Group" and the two become a group. Dropping a tab on a group's label moves the tab into that group. Dragging the group label moves the whole group. Alt+Shift+Left and Alt+Shift+Right still move the group one slot.

## How do I split a tab by dragging it to the edge

Drag a tab onto the left, right or bottom edge of the conversation. The edge says "Split left", "Split right" or "Split down". Letting go there splits the view. The keyboard split is unchanged.

## What happens if I drag a tab out of the window

Let go outside the window, or on the window's edge. An unpinned tab the desktop can move opens in a new window. A pinned tab stays. A browser has no second window, so the same release leaves the tab where it was and opens nothing. A chat, a place, a group or a queued row never leaves this window.

## How do I cancel a drag

Press Escape, or let go where nothing can take the drop. The tab, place, chat or row goes back and nothing is written. A release that never moved 4px is a click, not a cancelled drag.

## How do I reorder queued messages by dragging

Drag a queued row onto another queued row. It takes that row's place. A coarse pointer does not drag; Alt+Up and Alt+Down still move the focused row. Dropping a file from Finder into the composer is still an ordinary file drop.
