# Desktop Knows list presentation

## Why does Home show only three things a place knows?

The desktop Knows presentation model selects the top three lines by most recent use, then creation time. A line never used follows used lines. Equal timestamps retain the engine's stored order. “All” is available when more than three visible lines remain.

These are presentation helpers for Home integration. They do not themselves add a Home editor or deliver a prompt.

## What do the sources under things a place knows mean?

The source words preserve the engine's evidence: “You said in Launch post · Tue”, “Learned from 3 of your answers”, “brand-voice.md · you added”, or “You wrote”. Chat names come from real titles, weekdays from recorded source times, and answer counts from the engine. Missing evidence stays absent. File sources show the filename.

## Why is a replaced knowledge line struck out for seven days?

A replaced line is marked struck and says “Replaced Tue · kept for 7 days”, using its recorded replacement day. It remains in the presentation list for seven elapsed days, then disappears from both Home and All. This projection does not delete the engine's records.

## When does an unused knowledge line say still true?

The presentation model marks a live line “still true?” only after sixty elapsed days without use. Never-used lines age from creation time, or source time when creation is unknown. Unknown age and replaced lines produce no prompt. A recorded previous prompt suppresses another for sixty days. Delivery and answering belong to the separate prompt integration.
