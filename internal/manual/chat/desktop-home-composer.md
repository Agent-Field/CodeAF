# Starting a chat from a place's Home

## How do I start a chat from a place's Home on the desktop?

Type in the composer at the bottom of that place's Home and press Enter. codeaf
creates the chat in that place and opens it as a new tab immediately after Home,
focused. Home itself stays the Home tab.

The chat works in the place's first folder or repository. Other folders that
place lists stay attached; they do not become the working folder. A place with
no folder works where codeaf was started, and the desktop says nothing about a
folder in that case.

## What does the Home composer say?

On an empty place the composer says "Start the first chat in <place>", using
that place's name. Once the place has a chat, a child place, something that
needs you, a source, a decision or a line of knowledge, it says "Start something
in <place>". The model chip names the model the first message will run on.

## Does Enter on Home open a new tab, and what does Command Enter do?

Enter sends the words, files the chat in this place, and focuses the new tab
right after Home. Command Enter on a Mac, or Ctrl Enter on Linux, starts that
chat in the background and leaves you on Home, so you can start several in a
row. Shift Enter adds a line and does not send.

## Does Home turn into the chat?

No. Home never becomes a chat. The new tab is the conversation. Its title starts
as the first line you sent. codeaf replaces that with a short title after the
first answer, unless you renamed the tab yourself.
