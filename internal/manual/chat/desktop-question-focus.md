# Desktop question focus

## Why do desktop question buttons and fields have a ring when I use the keyboard?

Tab to an answer button or the optional "Say why (optional)" field in a desktop question card. Keyboard focus draws a 2px accent ring with a 4px soft halo beyond the ring, in light and dark appearance. The halo reaches 6px from the control's edge. Buttons and the optional field share this treatment.

## Does clicking the optional desktop question field show a focus ring?

Clicking "Say why (optional)" focuses the field without the keyboard ring. Move through the card with Tab to show keyboard focus again.
