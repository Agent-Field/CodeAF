# The line under a place name

## Why does home say deciding automatically

On a place's Home, under the place's name, one quiet line says how that place handles questions. A 12px sparkle sits in front of it, in the same quiet ink as the words. It is not a button.

When the place decides on its own, the line reads `Deciding automatically · 41 of 44 agreed this week`. The two numbers are this week: how many you agreed with, of how many it decided. A week with nothing agreed keeps the words `Deciding automatically` and leaves the numbers off. A zero is not written out.

## What does learning 14 of 20 agreed mean under the home title

While the place is still learning, the line under its name reads `Learning · 14 of 20 agreed`. It does not name the kind of question. The weakest kind's count stays off this line. The question in the tray shows that kind's own count.

Nothing agreed yet leaves the numbers off, so the line is just `Learning`.

## What does always asks you mean under the home title

A place set to always ask you shows `Always asks you` under its name. There is no count on that line.

## When is the place status line hidden

When the place has never been asked anything, the line under its name is not there. Nothing is drawn in its place.
