# Undo a running tab you just closed

## Command Z while the running-tab close toast is still up

Closing a tab that is still working does not stop that work. A toast names the tab and says “closed and still running”, with Stop it and Undo. Command Z on a Mac, or Ctrl Z on Linux, is that toast’s Undo while the toast is on screen: the tab comes back where it was and becomes the selected tab, and the toast leaves. A second Command Z does not open that tab again. The close was one step, and the toast spent it.

## Stop it on the closed-and-still-running toast

Stop it is the control that stops the work. Undo does not stop it, and neither does waiting until the toast leaves. Closing the tab never sends Stop by itself.
