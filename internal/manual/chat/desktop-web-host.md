# The web page on its sheet

## Does resizing the window, the split or the sidebar move the web page

In the desktop app the page sits on its own sheet inside the card: 8px in from
the sides and the bottom, corners of 8px, and the light page colour even when
the app is dark. Resizing the window, dragging the split divider, or hiding
the sidebar moves that one page with the sheet. A pane has one page. Switching
away hides it and keeps it, so coming back is the same page, not a new one.

## Does the filmstrip open a web page on the side cards

No. All tabs hides the page, and a filmstrip side card does not open one of
its own. The small cards are pictures of the tabs. Closing All tabs puts the
page back on the sheet.

## Closing a web tab closes its page

Closing the tab closes that page and no other. A conversation you started from
the page keeps running.

## A web tab opened in a split or moved to another window

Opening the tab in a split keeps the one page and moves it onto the sheet in
that split. Moving the tab to another window closes the page in the window
you left and opens the same address in the new window.
