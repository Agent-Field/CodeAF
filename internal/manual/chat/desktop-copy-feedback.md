# Desktop copy feedback

## Why does the desktop Copy button briefly show a check?

After Copy successfully writes the text to your clipboard, the button shows a check for 1.2 seconds, then returns to its copy icon. The text stays in your clipboard after the check disappears. Copying again starts a fresh 1.2-second confirmation. If the clipboard write fails, the button does not show a check or announce "Copied".

## Do desktop menus animate with reduced motion?

Menus and hover previews fade in from 98% scale at their anchor over 120 milliseconds. With reduced motion enabled in your system settings, they appear immediately at full size. There is no separate reduced motion switch in the desktop app.
