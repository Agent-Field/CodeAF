# Desktop Quick Look

## How do I preview a place without switching my tabs?

On a desktop Home, focus a place tile and press Space for Quick Look. A rail place row also offers Quick Look in its menu. The read-only sheet shows the place's swatch and name, its recap when the engine supplied one, up to three chats, and the child-place names after “Places:”. Missing recap, chats or child places draw nothing. Opening Quick Look does not change the window's place, tint or tabs.

## How do I close Quick Look with Space or Escape?

The Quick Look sheet says “Space to close”. Space or Escape dismisses it and returns focus to the tile or row that opened it. Clicking the scrim also closes it. Keyboard focus stays within the sheet while it is open. Chat rows are for reading; they do not open chats or offer editing actions.

## How do I go to the place from Quick Look?

Choose “Go to <name>” to dismiss the sheet and go to that place. “Open in new window” dismisses the sheet and asks the desktop shell to open the place in another window. A navigation action is absent when the host has no handler for it. The sheet fills windows that are 600 pixels wide or narrower.
