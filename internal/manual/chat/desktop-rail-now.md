# Desktop Now row

## How do I switch the desktop window to Now and my unplaced tabs?

Click Now in the desktop rail. Every open tab shows, unfiltered, a Focus on a place ends, and the frame goes back to graphite. No tab is opened, closed or moved. Places are libraries, so Now is the whole strip rather than a separate workspace.

## How do I command-click or middle-click Now to open a new window?

In the desktop app, Command-click (Control-click on Linux) or middle-click the Now row opens a new window on Now. The current window keeps its tabs. Now has no right-click menu.

## What does command-click Now do in a browser?

In a browser, Command-click or Control-click Now shows every tab, just like a plain click. The row offers no native new-window action. Middle-click has no new-window action in a browser.
