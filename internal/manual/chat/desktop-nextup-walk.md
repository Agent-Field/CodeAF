# Desktop Next up walk

## How do I walk through questions elsewhere with Next up?

Click the window-frame pill or press Command J (Control J on Linux) to start
Next up. The pill counts questions outside the conversation you are reading.
Blocking work comes first and irreversible actions come last. A popover item,
a banner, or a clicked system notification starts at that exact question.
A question already answered elsewhere is dropped without opening another card
in its place.

Each item opens its own conversation tab in its place, changes the window tint
and rail selection, and brings the question forward in the tray. Existing tabs
are reused. These visits do not send a chat message or start work.

## Does answering Next up load the next question? What does Skip do?

Answer through the ordinary question tray. Only an answer accepted by the engine
counts as answered. Next up then opens the next waiting question, including one
in another place. A refused answer stays on the card for another try.
Skip sends the current item to the back of this window's queue. It does not
answer, decline, or remove the question for another window. A question that
vanishes because somebody else answered it is removed from the walk and is not
counted as your answer.

## How do I exit Next up and get my draft and scroll back?

Escape or Command [ (Alt Left on Linux) exits Next up to the place and tab where
you started. The Back to button does the same. The original draft, route and
remembered scroll position return. An open menu or dialog keeps Escape for its
own dismissal first.

When the queue is finished, the end card reads "You're clear". It counts answers
accepted during this walk and running tasks reported across the engine's world
feed. Unknown or zero counts draw nothing. Back to returns to the start; work
continues running.
