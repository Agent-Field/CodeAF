# Desktop decision lines in the conversation

## Allowed automatically by a place — the line under the thing it answered

When a place answers a question for you, the conversation keeps a quiet “Allowed automatically” line saying so: “Allowed automatically by Marketing · You allowed go test here 6 times. · Why?”. A permission reads “Allowed”; any other kind of question reads “Decided”. The line sits under the call or question it answered, and “Why?” opens the card with the place that decided (By), the reason (Because), how sure it was (Sure) and whether the decision can be undone. A reason the engine did not record is left out of the line.

## Did 3 things — several automatic answers in one batch

When a place answers two or more questions in the same batch of work, the conversation shows one line, “Did 3 things” (the real number), with each answer listed beneath it. A plan card’s results group the same way. A lone answer is never wrapped in a group.

## Reopening a conversation — are the automatic lines still there

Yes. Each of these lines is saved with the conversation, so a reopened conversation draws the same lines, in the same groups, as the live one. A conversation saved before lines existed draws none, and nothing is made up for it.
