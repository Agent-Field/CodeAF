# Recently closed tabs

## Recently closed rows say closed 1h ago

On a new tab, a recently closed tab that matches what you typed shows how long
ago it closed. The line reads "closed" and a relative time, such as "closed 1
hour ago" or "closed just now". The time is the moment that tab was closed.

## Why does a recently closed tab just say closed

A closed tab that was saved before it recorded a close time has no age. Its
row says "closed".
