# Place menu on the desktop

## What is on the right-click menu of a place tile

Right-click a place tile. The menu is Go to (↵), Quick Look (Space), Open in new window (⌘↵ on a Mac, Ctrl ↵ elsewhere), then Rename, Tint, Add to another place…, Merge into…, and Pin to rail, then Archive and Delete place…. A line separates those three groups. When the engine has said how the place decides, Always ask me and Decision confidence… sit in their own group after Pin and before Archive.

Tint is five squares in the menu: Tide, Rose, Sage, Sand, Iris. Graphite is not one of them. The square this place already uses is marked. Left and right move that mark, and Enter sets it.

Delete place… is written in the danger colour. It has no icon. Choosing it asks first; it does not delete the place on the first click.

A row is missing when that action is not available. While the app is offline or still loading, only Go to, Quick Look and Open in new window remain.

## How do I pin a place to the rail or unpin it

Open the place menu and choose Pin to rail. That place then shows Unpin instead, and choosing Unpin takes it off the rail. Pin to rail is the keyboard path for the same thing as dragging the place onto the rail.

## Where is Add to another place on a place tile

Add to another place… is in the place menu, after Tint and before Pin to rail. It gives the place one more parent, the same result as dropping the place onto another tile. It does not move the place out of the parent it already has.

## Why is Delete place red words with no icon

Delete place… is the last row of the place menu. The words are the danger colour and there is no icon. Archive, just above it, keeps an ordinary icon and ordinary words. Choosing Delete place… opens the confirmation on that same page. The place stays until you confirm.

## What is on the Home page place actions menu

On a place's Home, the ⋯ beside the name is the place menu for the place you are already in. Go to and Quick Look are not in it. Open in new window, Rename, Tint, Add to another place…, Merge into…, Pin to rail or Unpin, Archive and Delete place… are. Now and All places have no ⋯ menu.

## Does the home tab place menu include archive and delete

Right-click the home tab. That menu has Open in new window, Rename, Tint, Add to another place…, Merge into…, and Pin to rail or Unpin, then Close place. Archive and Delete place… are not on the home tab. They stay on the Home page's ⋯ menu, where the delete confirmation is drawn. Go to and Quick Look are not on the home tab either.
