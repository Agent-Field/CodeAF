# Desktop place rail

## How do I switch places with the desktop rail hidden?

Hide or show the desktop rail with Command+S (Control+S on Linux); Command+B
also works. With the rail hidden, the pinned Home tab opens the place switcher.
It lists Now, Pinned places in your order, Open places, and All places.
Each place keeps its tint square; amber means something needs you and red means
something failed. Running work has no status dot. Parent names appear beside
nested places. Arrow keys move through the switcher, Enter goes to the chosen
place, and Escape dismisses it and returns focus to Home.

Control+0 goes to Now on macOS, and Control+1 through Control+9 go to places,
Pinned first followed by Open. Linux uses Alt+0 through Alt+9 for places.

## How do I reorder or pin places in the desktop rail?

Drag an Open place into Pinned to pin it. Drag within Pinned to reorder; the
insertion line shows where it will land. Drag a pinned place into Open to unpin.
The row's right-click menu also offers Pin or Unpin. On a pinned row, Alt+Up
and Alt+Down move it one position; the menu offers Move up or Move down where
there is a neighbouring position. A pinned parent does not pull in its children.

## How do I use the desktop rail with a keyboard?

Tab enters the rail at its focused or selected row. Up and Down move focus
without changing places. Home and End focus the first and last rows. Enter
goes to the focused place; Space opens its Quick Look when available.
Shift+F10 opens its menu: Go to, Quick Look, Open in new window, Pin or Unpin,
Rename, Tint, Close, and Close all others. Actions absent from the engine or
native bridge are not offered.

## What happens when I close a place in the desktop rail?

Open rows reveal a close button on hover or keyboard focus. Pinned rows have
no close button. Close all is on the Open heading. Closing detaches the tabs;
it does not stop work or delete chats. A closed place still running or waiting
stays muted in Open with “closed · still running” until the work settles.

## Can I drop a file or a tab onto the desktop rail?

The rail currently files chat membership drags into a place. Its own place rows
drag between Pinned and Open. File, folder, URL and strip-tab payloads are not
wired to rail filing yet. Use Add files or links on the place Home for sources;
use a chat's Add to a place… menu for membership. A file name alone is not
accepted as a filesystem path.
