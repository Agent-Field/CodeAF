# Desktop Quick Look sheet

## How do I close the Quick Look preview?

Quick Look is a small sheet, 420px wide, that appears over the window with a dim backdrop behind it. Press Space to close it, press Esc, or click the dimmed area outside it. The header says "Space to close". Focus goes back to the thing you pressed to get here.

## Why does Space type a space instead of closing Quick Look?

When the cursor is in a text field inside the sheet, Space is a character and the sheet stays open. Space also keeps its normal job on a focused button, link or menu. Anywhere else inside the sheet, Space closes it.

## Does the Quick Look sheet shrink on a small window?

Yes. It is 420px wide, or the window width minus 32px when the window is narrower. The body scrolls when the content is taller than the window. With reduced motion turned on in the system settings the sheet fades in without the small zoom.
