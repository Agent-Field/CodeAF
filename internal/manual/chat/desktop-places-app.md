# Desktop places on the design system

## Where is the places specimen on the desktop design system?

Open Design system in a development build. The Places section is the places specimen: tint squares, place tiles, rail rows, the Using chip, and a sample Home. The rail rows in that section are pictures. They do not switch the window.

## Are the tiles on the places specimen my real places?

No. The names, counts, and chats in the places specimen are fixtures. They are not your places and they make no engine calls. Your places stay in the side list of the window you are working in.

## Does opening the places specimen change the desktop window colour?

No. The window shell keeps the colour of the place you are in. Now and All places are graphite. A place you have gone to uses that place's tint. Opening Design system does not swap it.
