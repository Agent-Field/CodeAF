# The Inbox

## what the Inbox is — the first place the app opens, its six sections

The Inbox is where everything that needs you arrives, Personal and each organization you are signed in to. It is the first place the app opens. On the left it lists six sections, each with its count: "Waiting on you", "Due and late", "Sent back", "Comments for you", "Running for you" and "Waiting on others". Only Waiting on you is amber, because amber means something waits on you. A section with nothing in it shows no number. In the middle is the list for the section you chose; on the right, the item you selected, open. The Inbox opens with the first row selected. In Personal, under the six sections, "Chats asking you" lists the questions this computer's chats are waiting on, with their own search and Waiting, Later and Done today; a row opens the chat at its question.

## Waiting on you — Blocking work, Quick and can be undone, Can wait

Waiting on you lists every Ask whose decider is you, one row each: who asks, the question, the workstream, the asker and how long ago, or "Afi would answer: …" when Afi recommends an answer. Rows come in three groups:

- "Blocking work": Asks that other tasks wait on, the one blocking the most tasks first. The row says "blocks 6".
- "Quick, and can be undone": Asks whose answer can be undone, marked so by whoever raised them.
- "Can wait": the rest. The row says "nothing blocked".

Nothing is answered from the list. Select a row to read the Ask in full on the right: its reasoning, its answers ("Afi recommends" on the one Afi would pick, which comes first), and "Waiting on this" with the tasks it blocks. Answering opens the next row. With nothing left the list reads "Nothing is waiting on you."

## answer every quick Ask at once — Accept Afi's answer on all

The "Quick, and can be undone" group has "Accept Afi's answer on all 6" (the number is how many it holds). It answers each Ask in the group with the answer Afi recommends, as if you had pressed it, and keeps each answer's decision in its workstream's Decisions. An Ask with no recommended answer is left for you. Only Asks marked "can be undone" are in the group: an agent marks one only when its answer can honestly be undone with nothing lost but a little time, and an Ask a person raises is never marked unless they say so. Anything published, sent, paid for or against a decision always asks you one by one.

## undo an answer — Undo on the task

An answer to an Ask that can be undone stays undoable from its task until the task is done. On the task, "You answered “Rename”" has "Undo": the Ask opens again as it was asked, the task waits on it, and the decision your answer kept is withdrawn (it stays in Decisions, greyed). Only the person who answered can undo it ("only Santosh can undo their answer"). A done task is not undone this way: send it back instead ("this task is done: send it back instead"). An Ask not marked "can be undone" has no Undo ("that answer cannot be undone").

## put an Ask off until tomorrow, or hand it to another decider

Under the answers are "Later" and "Hand to someone else". Later puts the Ask away until tomorrow at 09:00; it leaves Waiting on you until then and is not answered. Hand to someone else picks one person in the workstream to decide it instead of you; the task, its owner and the question stay the same, the thread says "Santosh handed “…” to Ravi to decide", and the Ask moves to their Inbox. Only whoever decides an Ask hands it on, and only to someone in the workstream ("Lena is not in this workstream, so they cannot answer its Asks; ask someone who is").

## the box under an open Ask — Or tell Afi something else, Or reply to Santosh

Below an open Ask is one box. On an Ask Afi or a chat raised it reads "Or tell Afi something else" and sends your words to the agent on the task, as an answer in your own words. On an Ask a person raised it reads "Or reply to Santosh" and sends your words to them as your answer.

## Running for you — tasks you own that an agent runs, Take it back and Stop

Running for you lists the tasks you own that an agent is working on, across every workstream, in three groups: "Asking you" (the agent is paused on your answer), "Working" (with its current step, "rewriting the install step") and "Finished today". Selecting a row shows the task on the right: who does it, its facts, "Now" with its last steps, "Assumed so far" with what it decided without asking, and "Request". The box there talks to the agent on the task ("Tell your chat Docs something").

"Take it back" sets Done by to You and stops the agent; what it made stays in the task, and the task waits for you in Up next. Only the task's owner takes it back ("only the task's owner takes it back"). "Stop" ends the work: the task is stopped, and Start begins it again.

## the first day — Plans to start and Set up in the Inbox

On your first day the Inbox shows "Plans to start", one row per workstream whose plan from Afi has not started ("AgentField 0.9 launch: 6 first tasks"). Its pane is the plan with who does each task ("Start the first six tasks?"); "Start 5" starts what can run now, and "Change the plan" opens the workstream. Below it, for an organization's admin, "Set up" lists what is still to connect: "Connect a repository" ("The factory needs one before it can fix or build anything") and "Add what Afi should know". Each item leaves the group when it is done, or when you press "Not now". This is the only place set-up items appear; there is no separate set-up page.

## a teammate's first Inbox — who added you

Someone added to an organization opens on their Inbox with one line saying who added them ("Santosh added you to AgentField"), how many workstreams and which team, and "Got it" to put it away. Below it are their Asks and the tasks handed to them under "Handed to you". They see only the workstreams they are in, and their Afi and chats are private to them.
