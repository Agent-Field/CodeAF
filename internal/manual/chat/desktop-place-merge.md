# Merging two desktop places

## How do I merge two places in the desktop app

Open the place menu and choose Merge into…. The sheet is named Merge “Name” into…. Pick the place that should remain. That pick merges at once. A toast says Merged “Name” into “Survivor” and offers Undo. Undo puts both places back, including their chats, instructions and pin.

The place you opened the menu on is the one that goes away. You choose the one that stays. codeaf does not suggest a target.

## Which place is kept when I merge two desktop places

The place you pick in Merge “Name” into… is kept. The place named in that title is absorbed into it and removed. The sheet does not offer the absorbed place, anything inside it, or an archived place, because a place cannot sit inside itself. Merging a place into itself is refused.

## What happens to instructions, the model and the tint when two places merge

The kept place’s instructions stay first. The other place’s instructions are added after a blank line when they say something different. The same paragraph is not repeated. Sources are combined: the same kind and address is kept once, and the kept place’s copy stays.

The kept place’s model and permissions stay. A blank model or a blank permissions field is filled from the place that went away. The two are not mixed, and you are not asked to pick.

The kept place keeps its tint. Colours are not blended. A place that was inside the one that went away keeps the colour it was already showing.

Lines that place knows move to the place you kept. Their wording stays. Chats filed in the old place are filed in the one you kept. A chat that was already in both stays once, and its other places stay. No chat is deleted.

Places inside the one that went away become places inside the one you kept. If that would put a place inside itself, those places move up to the parents the old place had. The kept place also gains the old place’s parents, except a parent that would make a loop, which is skipped.

If only the old place was pinned to the rail, that pin moves to the place you kept. The place you kept keeps the tabs it already had. The old place’s saved tabs are not copied over; Undo brings them back with the place.

## Where is Merge into in the desktop place menu

Merge into… sits after Add to another place… and before Pin to rail. It is on the right-click menu of a place, on the Home page’s own menu, and on the Home tab’s place menu. The Home tab’s menu then has Close place. Archive and Delete place… stay on the Home page’s menu.

When the place cannot be written, Merge into… is absent. Now and All places have no place menu.

## Does merging a desktop place delete its chats

No chat is deleted. Chats move onto the place you kept, and a chat that already belonged to other places still belongs to them. History still has them. The window you merged from goes to the place you kept. Another window that was still showing the place that went away says That place no longer exists, so this window shows Now.

## What does Merge do on a desktop place that has not been touched

A place left untouched can offer Merge, Archive and Not now. Merge opens the same Merge “Name” into… sheet. It does not choose a place for you. Archive and Not now do not merge.
