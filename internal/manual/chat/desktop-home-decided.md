# Decisions the desktop home made automatically

## Where the desktop home lists decisions it made automatically

On a place's Home in the desktop app, decisions that place made for you are
listed under **Decided automatically · N**. N is how many there are. The list
shows the three newest. Each row is a check, the decision ("Allowed publishing
to staging"), a quiet line when one was recorded ("Launch post · you allowed
it twice"), and a short age when one was recorded ("1h"). A missing quiet line
or age is left out. A place with no such decision shows no heading and no rows.

## What All does beside Decided automatically

**All** sits on the right of that heading when more than three decisions are
listed. It opens the rest in place, newest first, up to 200. It does not open
another page. When every decision is already on screen, All is not shown.

## How to open why a desktop decision was made automatically

Click a row, or press Enter or Space on it. That opens **Why?** under the row.
The card is titled Decided automatically. It shows **By**, **Because** and
**Sure** for the fields that decision recorded, and leaves out a field it does
not have. Escape closes the card and puts focus back on the row. A press
outside the row closes it too. Up and Down move between the rows.

## How to show fewer decided rows on the desktop home

After All, the same control reads **Show fewer**. It folds the list back to
the three newest. The heading keeps the full count.
