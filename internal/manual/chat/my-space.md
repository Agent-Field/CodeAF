# My space — your own private place inside an organization

## What is My space — a private space for me inside the organization

Inside an organization window, My space is yours alone: your own documents, canvases, chats and agents about the organization's work. It sits first in the organization's list, above Workstreams. Nobody else in the organization sees it — not your colleagues and not the operator. It is made the first time you open it; there is nothing to set up, and it cannot be deleted.

My space is not Personal. Personal is your life outside any organization and lives on this computer; My space lives on the organization's server and runs on the organization's terms. The two never mix: nothing of Personal shows in an organization window, and nothing of My space shows in Personal.

## What My space holds — documents, canvases, chats and agents, but no tasks

My space holds documents and files, canvases, chats, and your own agents (its Agents tab; see "Making my own agent in an organization"). It holds no tasks: a task is a promise other people can see, so it belongs in a workstream. Asking for a task in My space is refused with "My space holds no tasks: a task belongs in a workstream, where others can see it."

My space cannot be shared, handed to someone else, renamed, archived or deleted, and no workstream can sit inside it. To share a document or file you made there, move it into a workstream (below); a chat is shared by a grant instead (see "Can I share a chat, a canvas or a workstream with a teammate"), because moving a chat does not share it.

## Chats in an organization run on the organization's server

In an organization window, New chat starts a chat in My space. It runs on the organization's server, never on this computer: close the laptop mid-answer and the chat finishes anyway, with what it made waiting in My space when you come back. It uses the organization's model key and the model the organization chose.

A chat in My space is private to you, like Afi's pane: nobody else lists or reads it, its name included, unless you share it. (A chat started inside a workstream is private the same way: its name, its owner and its state are listed to other people only after you share it, and never what is said.) Its title is its first message's first line. What the agent writes into documents and canvases lands in My space.

## Who pays for My space — the organization, under your name, up to a monthly limit

Everything My space spends is recorded under your name, and is paid with the organization's key (or with your own, for a hosted chat when you have stored one). The operator sets one monthly limit for everyone's space in Settings › Organizations (it starts at $20). When your space reaches it, its chats stop taking messages and say "Paused: this month's limit for your space is reached." It starts again on the first of the next month (UTC), or sooner if the operator raises the limit. Work in workstreams is not affected; it runs under each workstream's own budget.

## What the organization's admin can see of my space — totals only

The operator sees two numbers for each person's space: what it spent this month and how many things it holds. Never a title, a document, a chat or a word of what they say. The operator is not told a private workstream or a private canvas exists either — not even its name.

When the operator removes someone from the organization, everything that person owned, their space included, goes to the person the operator names. Their space arrives as one private workstream called "<name>'s space"; the heir keeps their own My space. That is the only way anyone else reaches it.

## Moving a document from My space into a workstream — Move to workstream, Undo

In My space, a document's or file's menu has **Move to workstream…**: pick one of the workstreams you can edit and the file goes into its Files. It is the one copy, not a duplicate. Once moved it belongs to that workstream: everyone in it sees and edits it, and its agent work is that workstream's spend.

A note says "Moved to Pantry" with **Undo** for 10 seconds; Undo brings it back into My space. The workstream's Decisions keep one line, "Priya moved “Launch plan” in from their space", and Undo withdraws it. After the note has gone, only whoever looks after that workstream can move the file out to a space again; you are told "moving a file back to your space once its Undo has gone needs whoever looks after this workstream".

A chat is not moved with a file's **Move to workstream…**: what a chat made moves that way, and the chat itself moves only as "Moving a chat between My space and a workstream" says, by its owner.

## What My space cannot do yet

My space holds no crews, and it has no Library of its own: the organization's Library is shared by everyone in it (see "The organization's Library"). Your Personal agents and Library never appear in an organization window.

## What is all my work — Afi looks across workstreams from My space

In My space, Afi's pane says **Looking at: All my work**. Ask “what is all my work?” to read across the workstreams you can access in this organization, including their tasks, owners, state and waiting questions. This is a read-only view: it does not let Afi change another workstream from My space. Workstreams you cannot access and other people's private spaces stay private. Your Personal work outside this organization is separate.

A named chat in My space still reads its own space. The wider view belongs to Afi's pane.
