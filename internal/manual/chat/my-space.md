# My space — your own private place inside an organization

## What is My space — a private space for me inside the organization

Inside an organization window, My space is yours alone: your own documents, canvases and chats about the organization's work. It sits first in the organization's list, above Workstreams. Nobody else in the organization sees it — not your colleagues and not the operator. It is made the first time you open it; there is nothing to set up, and it cannot be deleted.

My space is not Personal. Personal is your life outside any organization and lives on this computer; My space lives on the organization's server and runs on the organization's terms. The two never mix: nothing of Personal shows in an organization window, and nothing of My space shows in Personal.

## What My space holds — documents, canvases and chats, but no tasks

My space holds documents and files, canvases, and chats. It holds no tasks: a task is a promise other people can see, so it belongs in a workstream. Asking for a task in My space is refused with "My space holds no tasks: a task belongs in a workstream, where others can see it."

My space cannot be shared, handed to someone else, renamed, archived or deleted, and no workstream can sit inside it. To share something you made there, move it into a workstream (below).

## Chats in an organization run on the organization's server

In an organization window, New chat starts a chat in My space. It runs on the organization's server, never on this computer: close the laptop mid-answer and the chat finishes anyway, with what it made waiting in My space when you come back. It uses the organization's model key and the model the organization chose.

A chat is private to you, like Afi's pane: nobody else lists or reads it. Its title is its first message's first line. What the agent writes into documents and canvases lands in My space.

## Who pays for My space — the organization, under your name, up to a monthly limit

Everything My space spends is paid with the organization's key and recorded under your name. The operator sets one monthly limit for everyone's space in Settings › Organizations (it starts at $20). When your space reaches it, its chats stop taking messages and say "Paused: this month's limit for your space is reached." It starts again on the first of the next month (UTC), or sooner if the operator raises the limit. Work in workstreams is not affected; it runs under each workstream's own budget.

## What the organization's admin can see of my space — totals only

The operator sees two numbers for each person's space: what it spent this month and how many things it holds. Never a title, a document, a chat or a word of what they say. The operator is not told a private workstream or a private canvas exists either — not even its name.

When the operator removes someone from the organization, everything that person owned, their space included, goes to the person the operator names. Their space arrives as one private workstream called "<name>'s space"; the heir keeps their own My space. That is the only way anyone else reaches it.

## Moving a document from My space into a workstream

A document or file in My space moves into a workstream from its menu (Move to…), the same way files move between workstreams. Once moved, it belongs to that workstream: everyone in it can see it, and its agent work is that workstream's spend. A chat never moves; what the chat made does.

## What My space cannot do yet

My space does not hold your own agents or crews yet, and an organization has no shared Library yet. Your Personal agents and Library never appear in an organization window.
