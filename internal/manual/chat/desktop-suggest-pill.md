# The group suggestion pill

## Where is the group suggestion pill

The group suggestion pill sits at the top centre of the conversation card, over the page you are reading. It appears when three or more open tabs share one workspace folder, the same rule as a group suggestion for tabs on one workspace. Fewer than three, or tabs on different folders, draws nothing. A pinned tab and a tab already in a group are left out of the count.

## What does the group suggestion pill say

It reads "Group the 3 bench tabs as bench?" when three tabs share a folder named bench. The folder name is the only name: it is the word in the sentence and the medium-weight name after "as". Group is the accent button. The × is Dismiss.

## How do I use the Group button on the suggestion pill

Group makes one group of those tabs, named for the folder, and the pill goes away. The same set of tabs is not asked again until you open the app again. Nothing about the choice is saved.

## How do I dismiss the group suggestion pill

Dismiss hides the pill and does not group the tabs. That set stays quiet for the rest of this time the app is open. Opening the app again can ask once more. Group and Dismiss are ordinary buttons you can reach with Tab.

## Does the group suggestion pill wrap on a narrow window

At 600 pixels wide and below, the pill wraps inside the card, 16 pixels in from the card's edges together. Group and Dismiss stay visible. Wider than that, the pill stays one line.
