# Desktop Foundations specimen

## Where can I see the desktop Foundations design tokens?

Open Design system in the desktop app and find Foundations. Its nine sections
show two rhythms, place tints, light and dark colour ramps, type, space and
radius and depth, status marks, step categories, motion, and materials.
Everything here is a fixture: the running marks, counts and elapsed times do
not describe your conversations and make no engine calls.

## Why are there light and dark swatches together in Foundations?

Each colour ramp has its own Light cell and Dark cell, so changing the app's
appearance does not change which half you are comparing. The six place tints
also appear in both appearances. Graphite is the neutral tint used by Now.

## Why does the Foundations shimmer or running dot move?

The motion specimen demonstrates the two shared loops: the shimmer on one live
step and the breathing dot beside the running count. Reduced motion makes both
still. The marks in the status section stay still. The three material drawings
compare opaque frame, chosen tinted vibrancy, and clear glass; they do not
change your window's native material. Content stays opaque.
