# Desktop rail drag destinations

## How do I drag the first place into Pinned or unpin the last place?

Drag a place row with the pointer. While the drag is in progress, an empty Pinned
or Open section appears with a landing area. Drop into Pinned to pin the place;
drop a pinned place into Open to unpin it. The temporary section disappears when
you release the pointer or press Escape. Empty sections stay hidden at rest.

Dragging a row within Pinned reorders it. A drop writes once. An unsupported
internal drag does nothing. Pinning and unpinning do not close conversation tabs:
places are libraries and the desktop keeps one tab set.
