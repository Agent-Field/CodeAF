# The desktop place home

## Since yesterday, the line under a chat, and chats that are not in any place

On a desktop place home, "Since yesterday" (or "Since last week") is a short note of what changed after you last opened that place. It is at most two sentences, and each sentence is a recap a conversation had already saved. Nothing new is written for it. If you have never opened the place, or nothing with a recap has changed since, the note is not shown.

The line under a chat is that same saved recap, the one History already shows. A conversation that has not saved one has no second line. The title is not copied in to stand there.

All places lists conversations that are not filed in any place. One place's own home does not list them.

## Why a child place shows on the parent

A desktop place home lists what needs you, what is running, and what failed in that place and in the places inside it. A row names the place the conversation is filed in, so a question in a child place still names that child when you are looking at the parent. A place can sit in more than one parent; its tile says "also in" and the other parents' names. The breadcrumb follows the first parent.
