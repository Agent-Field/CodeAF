# Suggested place card

## Where is the suggested place card, and what do Create and Not now do?

A suggested place card appears on All places only when the engine proposes a new place for at least five chats. It says Suggested place, then the name the engine chose, then the engine's own line about those chats. Create makes that place and files the chats. Not now hides that card for 30 days, including after you reload. Fewer than five chats, or a proposal with no name, draws no card. codeaf does not invent the group and does not call a model to write it.

## Why is there a sparkles line about chats that belong in a place?

On All places, a sparkles line can say some chats look like they belong in a place that already exists, with Move them and Not now. That sentence is the engine's proposal. Move them files those chats, and you can undo it. Not now hides that proposal for 30 days. If the engine sent nothing, the line is not there.

## What is the quiet merge or archive line on All places and in go to?

A place left untouched for 60 days can be offered on All places and in go to, with Merge, Archive and Not now. Days are 24 hours each, and the day it reaches 60 counts. A visit, the day it was created, or chat activity the engine reports keeps it current. Pinned, archived, running and waiting places are left out. An unknown age is not guessed. Not now hides that place for 30 days. It can come back when those 30 days are up, if the place is still idle. The line does not merge, archive or delete anything by itself.
