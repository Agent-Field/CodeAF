# Desktop shared primitives

## Where can I see the desktop shared primitives?

Open Design system in the desktop app and find Shared primitives. The section
shows the shared controls in the states they have: rest, a hover hint on the
icon buttons, selected, disabled, a keyboard focus target, and the same set
inside a 320px container. That includes the choice menu, the search field,
filter tabs, the plain Suggested tag, the boxed and inline key hint, place
tint squares at each size, the live-step shimmer, the breathing dot, and the
stop square. Tray buttons are the taller ones. Everything here is a fixture.
The counts and the Quick Look are not your work and make no engine calls.

## How do I try a six second and ten second toast on the design system?

On that Shared primitives section, Show a 6 second toast and Show a 10 second
toast post a sample notice. The shorter one uses the usual toast time. The
longer one uses the ten-second undo time. Each offers Undo. A newer sample
replaces the one on screen. They are fixtures on their own channel, so they
do not call the engine and they do not replace a toast from something you
just did. Open Quick Look on the same section opens a Reading preview. Space
or Escape closes it.

## What does the tray size button look like on the design system?

Shared primitives draws tray buttons beside the shorter controls. The tray
size is the taller target used for an answer in the tray. Disabled tray and
Loading tray are on the Disabled row. Loading tray stays inactive while it
is marked busy. The plain Suggested tag is ink with no fill. The key hint
is a box in one row and the quieter inline spelling in another.
