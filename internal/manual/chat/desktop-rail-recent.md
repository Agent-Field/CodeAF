# Recent in the desktop rail

## Where are my recent chats and the tabs I just closed

The left rail ends with a Recent section under your places. It lists up to five things you closed in this window, newest first: chats, tasks, files, terminals, web pages, the History tab and a place's Home. Each row shows what kind of tab it was, its title, a small place tint if it belongs to one, how long ago you closed it ("now", "5m", "3h", "2d") and a coloured dot only if the work is running, needs you or failed. A tab you have open again is not listed, and a chat closed twice appears once.

## How do I get back a tab I closed or reopen a conversation I closed by accident

Click its row in Recent. The tab opens right next to the one you are on, with its draft, scroll position and where you had drilled to, and the row leaves Recent because the tab is open again. Command-click (Ctrl-click on Linux and Windows) opens it behind the one you are on. Command-Shift-T does the same for the newest closed tab. Recent and Command-Shift-T read the same list, as does the new-tab search under "closed".

## Forget a recent tab or clear Recent

Hover a row and press its x, or right-click it and choose Forget, to take it off Recent. That only removes it from the list: the conversation is never deleted and stays in History (Command-Y). Right-click also offers Open, Open in split and Clear recent, which forgets every row. Forgotten tabs can no longer be reopened with Command-Shift-T.

## Hide or fold Recent

The Recent header folds the section; codeaf remembers the fold for this window. With no closed tabs the section is not drawn at all. With the rail collapsed, nothing shows; in a narrow window it is in the navigation drawer. The link at the foot of the section, "History", opens the History tab. Recent keeps the last 20 closed tabs across a reload or relaunch and shows five. Tabs archived for being idle also appear here. It cannot be dragged into the tab strip or a split yet; use Open in split from the right-click menu.
