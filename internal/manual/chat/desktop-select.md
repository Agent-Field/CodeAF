# Desktop choice menu

## How do I pick from a desktop dropdown

A choice control opens a menu of the real options. The theme gear in the rail and in Appearance is one of these. Click it, or focus it and press Enter. The current choice shows a check. Click a row, or highlight one and press Enter, to choose it. A row that cannot be chosen is dimmed and does not take the highlight.

## What do Home, End, Enter and Escape do in a desktop dropdown

With the menu open, Home highlights the first choice and End highlights the last. A dimmed choice is skipped. Enter chooses the highlighted row and closes the menu. Escape closes the menu without changing the choice and puts focus back on the control.

## Why does the desktop dropdown highlight a whole row

The highlighted row is a soft fill. The words stay the same colour. The check on the current choice is the only mark that it is chosen. Moving the highlight does not colour the words.
