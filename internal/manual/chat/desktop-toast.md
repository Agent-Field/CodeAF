# Desktop toast notifications

## Why did the desktop toast disappear or replace another notification?

The desktop shows one toast notification at the bottom centre of each window.
A newer toast replaces the previous one. Notifications normally disappear after
six seconds; a delete notification can last ten seconds. They belong to the
window where the action happened and are not saved across a restart.

## Can I pause a desktop toast while reaching for Undo?

Hover over the toast or put keyboard focus on one of its actions to pause its
timer. The timer resumes with its remaining time after both the pointer and
keyboard focus leave. An action that is still running also keeps the toast
visible. Undo appears when the change offers an undo operation; other actions
come from the operation itself. Choosing a successful action dismisses the toast.
If the action fails, its explanation replaces the sentence and remains available
for a fresh duration.

## How do I dismiss a desktop toast with Escape?

Press Escape while keyboard focus is inside the toast to dismiss it. Escape
outside the toast does not dismiss it. A toast does not take keyboard focus
when it appears. Screen readers receive a polite status announcement.

## Why is the desktop toast message cut short in a narrow window?

The toast stays one line and shortens its message with an ellipsis before its
action buttons. At window widths of 600 pixels or less it leaves 12 pixels at
each side. Reduced motion removes its entrance movement and exit fade.
