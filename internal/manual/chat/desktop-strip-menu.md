# Desktop tab strip background menu

## How do I right-click empty tab strip space in the desktop app?

Right-click empty space in the desktop tab strip to open "Tab strip actions".
The shared menu offers "New tab", "Reopen closed tab", and "Show all tabs",
with shortcuts for your platform. Right-clicking a tab or group keeps its own menu.
Opening the menu makes no engine or provider call.

## How do I open the desktop tab strip background menu with Shift F10?

Tab to the "Tab strip actions" button, which is visually hidden in the strip's
empty spacer. Press Shift+F10 or the context-menu key. Arrow keys move through
the shared menu, Enter chooses an action, and Escape dismisses it.

## Why is Reopen closed tab disabled in the desktop strip background menu?

"Reopen closed tab" is disabled when the current workspace has no closed tabs.
Otherwise it restores the most recently closed tab. "New tab" opens a quiet
local tab; "Show all tabs" opens the existing overview. On macOS their shortcuts
are Command+T, Command+Shift+T, and Command+Shift+backslash. Other platforms use
Ctrl+T, Ctrl+Shift+T, and Ctrl+Shift+A.
