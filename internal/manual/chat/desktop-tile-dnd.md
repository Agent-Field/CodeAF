# Dragging place tiles

## How do I drag a chat onto a place tile

Drag a chat, or a place tile, onto another place tile. The tile you are over says "Add here". Letting go there adds the chat to that place, or adds that place as one more parent, and leaves every other place it was already in. Letting go off a tile changes nothing. A place tile will not say "Add here" for itself.

## How do I move a chat or a place with the option key

Hold Option while you drop. Off a Mac that key is Alt. The tile still says "Add here"; the words do not change. The drop moves instead of adding. A chat leaves the place you dragged it from and is filed in the tile you dropped it on. A place's only parent becomes that tile. Letting go off a tile still changes nothing.

## How do I add a place to another place without dragging

You do not have to drag. On a place tile, right-click or press Shift+F10 and choose "Add to another place…". That adds a parent and leaves the ones the place already has. On a chat, the same keys open "Add to a place…", which files the chat and leaves the places it is already in.
