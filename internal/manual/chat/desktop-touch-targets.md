# Desktop touch targets

## How do I close a tab with my finger?

On a touch screen the open tab keeps its close mark visible. That mark is a 24px target, so a finger can hit it. Tap it to close the tab. The work keeps running unless you choose Close and stop from the tab's menu. A mouse still shows the smaller mark, and only while the pointer is on the tab or the tab is the open one.

## How do I open a tab menu without a right click?

Press and hold the tab for half a second. The same menu a right-click opens appears at the bottom of the tab. A short tap still selects the tab and does not open the menu. Holding the close mark closes the tab; it does not open the menu. Letting go early, or sliding off the tab, opens nothing. A mouse hold does not open the menu, so dragging a tab still moves it.

## How do I open a group menu on a tablet?

Press and hold the group label for half a second. The group menu opens. The group does not collapse from that hold. A short tap still collapses or expands the group.

## Why are the sidebar rows taller on my phone?

On a touch screen each row in the desktop sidebar is 40px tall, so a finger can hit Now, a place, and All places. With a mouse those rows stay 32px. Press and hold a place row for half a second to open that place's menu. A short tap still goes to the place. Now has no menu.

## How do I resize a split with my finger?

The divider between split panes has a 16px hit area on a touch screen, wider than the thin line a mouse uses. Drag that area to resize. The line stays in the middle of the area.
