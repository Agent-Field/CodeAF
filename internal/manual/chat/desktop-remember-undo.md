# Remember and Undo in the desktop transcript

## Remember something in Marketing: Saved to Marketing

Saying “Remember…” in a chat saves a line in the place selected by the engine.
The desktop shows the engine's confirmation, for example
“Saved to Marketing: Run the suite before launch · Undo”, as a quiet system note.
The name and text come from the saved record; the desktop does not choose a place.

## Undo a remembered line: Removed

Click Undo beside “Saved to…” to remove that exact saved line. The button is disabled
while the engine answers. After removal succeeds, the confirmation becomes “Removed”.
If removal fails, the engine's explanation appears and Undo remains available.
Undo refuses a line that has since been edited or replaced, preserving that newer work.
