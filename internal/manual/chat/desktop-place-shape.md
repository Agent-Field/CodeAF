# How a place changes shape

## Folded into the place above

A place that holds at least 70 of every 100 chats in the place above it is folded into that place. A place that is the only one inside another is the same rule: it holds all of them, so it folds too. The chats stay in the place above. The folded place is offered first, and it is still there until you accept.

## Fewer than four chats

A place with fewer than four chats does not stay a place. Its chats stay with the place above it. When the place is at the top, those chats stay in Now. A smaller place inside it is settled first, so the place above is counted with those chats already included.

## A new place above several

When the top of the library has more places than it can show, codeaf may offer one new place above a group of them. The offer waits for a yes. A group that would hold more than 40 of every 100 chats still in a place is not offered. A group whose places are too alike to be worth separating is not offered either.

## Became a view

A place that is one kind of activity, rather than one thing you would go to, becomes a view on the place above it. The chats stay in the place above. If that question goes unanswered, the place stays a place. Silence does not turn it into a view.

## How deep places go

A place can be split while two or more groups inside it are each large enough to be their own place. That stops at six levels. A place that is not already three levels down is left whole.
