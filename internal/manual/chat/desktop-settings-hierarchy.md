# Desktop settings for places

## How many places can codeaf create in the desktop app

Settings, under Places organization, limits only places created from codeaf's offers. The defaults are 6 top-level places, 8 under one parent, and 30 in all. Each row says "Provisional default" because those numbers are engineering choices. Places you make yourself are not counted against them. A number outside the row's range is not saved.

## How deep can a desktop place be

The same section has "Deepest level for a new place". A top-level place is level 1. The default is 3 levels, and the row says places you make yourself can go deeper. The control only limits places created from codeaf's offers. You can set it from 1 to 6. The graph itself still allows a deeper place that you create.

## Can I put fifty reports under one place

Yes. Fifty reports under one parent is an ordinary place a person makes, and Settings does not stop it. The "8 under one parent" row counts only places created from codeaf's offers.

## What are the desktop settings categories

The Models part of Settings is four sections, in this order: Conversation and tasks, Naming and summaries, Places organization, and Memory, routing and safety. Pinned models come first. Provider key, Appearance and Engine come after. There is no Permissions section on this page yet. The place limits above sit in Places organization, after the Chat filing and Place suggestions rows, as one list.
