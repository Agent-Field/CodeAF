# Desktop: steer a place in your own words

## How do I organize this place by month or by project

On a place Home, use "Organize this by…", the field named "Steer this place", and press "Apply". The request applies to the place you are looking at. Try "organise this by month" or "by project".

By month adds views without moving chats or creating sub-places. By project or repository can offer sub-places when enough related chats belong together. "by kind of work" can offer sub-places by the work those chats describe. The chosen arrangement is saved for that place.

## Can I keep papers separate from code or flatten this

Try "keep papers separate from code" to offer separate destinations, or "flatten this" to offer folding the children into their parent. A structural change waits in the organizing plan: open the organizing "Review" in Settings to inspect it. You can leave out changes before "Accept". A request understood by the engine is not a promise that enough chats exist to offer a split.

## What if codeaf does not understand how I want this place arranged

An unrecognized request leaves the place unchanged and says "I did not understand that; try 'by month' or 'by project'". An empty field cannot be applied. A successful request clears the field and rereads the place's views. Errors stay beside the field so you can edit the request or try again.
