# Desktop council view

## What does a council discussion look like in the desktop app?

A council is two places talking a question through. In the desktop chat each
place's turn is a small tint square in that place's colour, the place's name in
semibold, and what it said beneath. There are no bubbles: you are reading, not
being answered. Messages are at most 560 pixels wide.

## What is the Decided card at the end of a council?

When the places settle, the discussion ends in a soft card that reads
“Decided: …” with the agreed sentence. A second, dimmer line gives the receipt:
whether both places agreed, how many turns it took, what it cost, and which
places it was filed in, for example “Agreed by both places · 2 turns · $0.01 ·
filed in Marketing and Config parser”. A fact the engine did not record is left
out of that line rather than shown as zero.

## Can I steer or add to a council discussion?

Yes. Under the discussion it says “You can read and steer a council like any
chat. Writing here pauses it until you send.” The message box reads “Add to this
discussion”. Your messages do not count as council turns, but what the places
spend replying to you does count towards the council's cost.
