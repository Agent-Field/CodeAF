# Desktop tab selection and middle-click close

## How do I select several desktop tabs for grouping?

Command-click a strip tab on macOS, or Control-click on Linux, to toggle its selection for grouping. Selected tabs have a soft field fill and the screen reader description “selected for grouping”. This selection belongs to this window and is not restored after reload. It does not switch the active conversation. Command-G (Control-G on Linux) groups the active tab with the selected tabs.

## How do I clear selected desktop tabs?

Plain-click a strip tab or press Escape to clear the grouping selection. Escape clears the selection without switching the active tab.

## Does middle-click close a desktop tab or stop running work?

Middle-click closes an unpinned strip tab like its ×. Pinned tabs and the place Home stay open. Closing a running tab keeps its work running and shows “closed and still running” with Stop it and Undo. Stop it explicitly stops the work; Undo reopens the tab. Middle-click never sends Stop, even while Alt is held.
