# Desktop live step title

## Why does the running step title shimmer?

In desktop conversations, only the last running step title in the expanded work
block shimmers. The quiet sweep uses normal text ink and repeats every 2.4 seconds.
Earlier running steps and steps preparing to start do not shimmer. A title stays
on one line and shows an ellipsis if it is too long.

## Why does the step clock stop when work finishes?

When a step settles, its shimmer stops. A completed or stopped title uses quiet
ink; a failed title keeps its darker ink and red state mark. The clock shows the
recorded duration and no longer advances. An unknown duration shows nothing.

## Can I stop the live step animation with reduced motion?

With system reduced motion enabled, the live step title is still, in secondary
text ink. Its clock continues while it runs and freezes when it settles.
