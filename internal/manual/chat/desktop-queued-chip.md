# Desktop queued chip

## The N queued chip

While a turn is running, a message you queue waits in a row above the composer.
Scroll up more than one screen from the latest reply and those rows fold into a
chip in the composer toolbar, just after the model name. The chip says how many
are waiting, in the form "2 queued" (one waiting says "1 queued"). Click the
chip and the conversation returns to the bottom, where the rows are back. Nothing
queued means no chip and no rows. The chip is quiet text; it does not change
colour to say the messages are waiting.

## I scrolled up and the queued messages disappeared

They are on the "N queued" chip in the composer, after the model name. Click that
chip to jump back to the latest reply. The rows show again once you are back at
the bottom, and they also stay put if you have only moved up by less than a
screen. Scrolling back down yourself does the same thing as the chip.
