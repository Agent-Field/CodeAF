# Desktop conversation tab focus

## How do I switch background conversations with the keyboard?

Focus a conversation tab in the desktop strip, then use Left or Right to select the adjacent visible tab. Home selects the first visible tab and End selects the last. Focus stays on the selected tab, including when its saved conversation attaches to the engine, so another arrow key can continue along the strip. Switching tabs does not stop background work.

## Why does the composer not take focus when I use the tab strip?

A focused tab keeps the keyboard while another conversation opens. Its Message field does not automatically take focus from a tab control. Move focus to Message when you want to type. When the composer opens without a focused tab control, its normal automatic focus still applies.
