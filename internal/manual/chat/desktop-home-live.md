# Desktop Home Live work

## What does Live on a place Home show?

The desktop Home's "Live" section lists work from the engine's place feed, including running work whose tab you closed. Closing a tab detaches its view; it does not stop the work. The heading disappears when there are no Live rows.

A running row has a small accent glyph and a neutral shimmering title. A question waiting for you has an amber glyph and the words "needs you". State words stay neutral in light and dark appearance. Reduced motion leaves the title still.

## How do I reopen closed running work from Home Live?

Click a Live row, or focus it and press Enter or Space. An open conversation is selected. A closed conversation still in the workspace's closed-tab history reopens in its recorded position with its saved draft and view state. When that history no longer contains it, the saved conversation opens in a new tab. If the engine has not supplied its saved session file, the Home shows why it cannot open it.

## Does Home Live show discussion topics and council turns?

Live rows can display a supplied discussion topic and a supplied count such as "2 of 6 turns". The current place feed does not send council turn counts; the desktop leaves those counts absent rather than guessing them. Questions and running-step details come from the engine feed. No example council, topic, or permission is inserted into a real Home.
