# Desktop collapsed tab groups

## How do I collapse and expand a desktop tab group?

Click a group label or right-click it and choose “Collapse”. The collapsed label stays in the strip with its member count, even when the active conversation is outside that group. Hidden members stay in the group; collapsing does not close tabs or stop work. If the active tab belongs to the group, it stays visible beside the label.

Click the label again or right-click it and choose “Expand” to show its tabs. Reduced motion keeps the same controls and behavior without the transition.

## How do I use a desktop tab menu with the keyboard?

Focus a tab and press Shift+F10 to open its menu. Up and Down move between actions. Right opens a submenu such as “Add to group”; Left returns to its parent action. Escape closes the menu and returns focus to the tab. Choosing “New group…” opens a naming dialog; save the name before using the new group's menu.
