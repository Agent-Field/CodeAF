# Desktop conversation header

The bar across the top of a desktop conversation shows its name, what is happening in it, and the panel toggle.

## Rename a conversation in the desktop app

Double-click the conversation's name in the header, or focus it and press Enter or F2, and it becomes a text field with the old name selected. Enter saves the new name, Esc keeps the old one. Names are at most 80 characters, and an empty or unchanged name changes nothing. A conversation that has no name yet cannot be renamed from the header.

## What the running and need-you counts in the header do, and why the running dot pulses

"N running" and "N need you here" sit at the right of the header. Clicking "N running" opens the tasks view filtered to running work; clicking "N need you here" jumps to the first question waiting in this conversation. A count that is zero is not drawn. The small dot beside "N running" breathes slowly while work runs, and sits still when the system asks for reduced motion. In a narrow pane (600 pixels or less) the counts are hidden.
