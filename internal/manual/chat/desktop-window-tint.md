# Desktop window tint

## Why did the window colour change when I went to a place?

Going to a place swaps the desktop window frame to that place's tint at once.
There is no fade. The frame and the accent use the place's colour: tide, iris,
rose, sand, sage, or graphite. A place inside another shows the colour it
inherited, until that place picks a colour of its own. A place the app has not
loaded yet leaves the frame untinted.

## What colour is Now?

Now is graphite. All places is graphite too. Graphite is the neutral. It is
also the colour a place shows when nobody has chosen a tint.

## Does the Mac title bar change colour with the place?

No. On a Mac the overlay title bar and the window's vibrancy stay as they are.
On Linux the window stays a normal decorated window. The tint is the frame
drawn in the page. The words in the title bar are not recolored.

## Why doesn't the window fade when I switch places?

The frame takes the new colour in the same moment as the place. Nothing
animates that change.
