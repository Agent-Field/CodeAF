# Desktop place picker

## How do I add a conversation to a place, or add a place to another place, in the desktop app?

"Add to a place…" on a conversation, "Add to another place…" on a place, and "Add to place" on a History row all open the same sheet as the go-to palette, in pick mode. The field asks the question ("Add to a place, or create one"), the list is Recent and the All tree, and typing searches by name and path. Press ↵ or click a row to choose it; the thing is added there and a toast says so. Esc or a click outside closes the sheet and changes nothing.

## Which places can I not choose when adding to a place?

Places the thing is already in are left out of the list, so a conversation cannot be added twice to the same place. When you add a place under another parent, the place itself, its descendants and its current parents are left out too. Archived places are never listed.

## Can I create a new place while picking one?

Yes. Type a name that matches nothing and the only row is Create “name”; ↵ or ⌘/Ctrl N makes the place and adds the thing to it in one step. If the picker was opened somewhere that cannot create places, choosing the row closes the sheet and does nothing. ⌘/Ctrl ↵ (open in new window) is not offered in pick mode, because nothing is being opened.
