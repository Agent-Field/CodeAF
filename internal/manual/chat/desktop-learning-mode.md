# When a place decides on its own

## When does a place start deciding for me

A new place watches first. Each kind of question starts in learning: a shell read, a git command, a choice, a judgement. The proposal is the choice already selected, where the question would have been asked. Agreeing means you chose that proposed key. Any other key is overruled.

A kind starts deciding on its own once 18 of the last 20 answers agreed with the proposal. Fewer than 20 answers keeps it learning, even when every one agreed. 17 of 20 stays learning. One kind graduating does not decide the others. `permission:shell-read` and `permission:git` each keep their own count.

## What does 14 of 20 agreed mean

While a kind is learning, its progress is counted toward the 20 answers it needs: 14 agreements is "14 of 20". The place line is "Learning · 14 of 20 agreed". Nothing agreed yet draws no count.

## I overturned one decision why is it asking again

Overturning a decision that place had made drops only that kind back to learning and forgets the 20 it had earned. It has to agree again, 18 of the last 20, before that kind decides on its own. A later run of answers you overruled does not do this. Only the overturn does.

## Why is this place still learning after I agreed seventeen times

Seventeen agreements are not enough. The kind stays in learning until 18 of the last 20 answers agreed, and until 20 answers exist at all. Agreeing every time for 18 answers, with two still to come, keeps it learning.

## Does overturning a git permission make it ask about shell commands too

No. Overturning a git permission puts `permission:git` back in learning. `permission:shell-read`, and any choice or judgement that had already graduated, keeps deciding.
