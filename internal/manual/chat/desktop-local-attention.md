# Desktop chat header attention

## What does needs you here count in the desktop chat header?

The chat header says “1 needs you here” or “N need you here”. It counts pending questions in this chat, including questions from its tasks. Questions in other conversations do not contribute. With no pending questions, the count disappears.

## How do I open the first question from the desktop chat header?

Click “N need you here” to bring this chat's tray into view on its first question. This does not open the Tasks page. The running count opens Tasks filtered to running work.

## What is the Next up progress chip beside the desktop chat title?

The header supports a “Next up” chip with muted progress such as “1 of 5”. It appears when the window's walker supplies progress for this conversation; without walking progress it is absent. The chip itself is a label, not a button.
