# Deleting a desktop place

## How do I delete a place on the desktop

On All places, or on a place's Home, open the place menu and choose Delete place….
The page itself asks, on one line. It does not open a dialog.

Cancel is the quiet button. Delete place is the danger button. Escape, or
Cancel, leaves the place where it was. Delete place stays hidden until codeaf
has said what the delete would change.

## What does chats become unplaced and places move up mean

The line names the place and the counts. Fourteen chats and three child places
read: Delete “Reading”? 14 chats become unplaced · 3 places move up. No chat
is deleted.

One chat is "1 chat becomes unplaced". One child place is "1 place moves up".
A count of zero is left off the line. A chat that also sits in another place
keeps that place, so it is not counted as unplaced.

Child places move up to the deleted place's parents. A child of a top-level
place becomes top-level. The chats stay in History.

## Undo deleting a desktop place within 10 seconds

After Delete place, a toast says Deleted “Name”. No chat was deleted. and
offers Undo for 10 seconds. Undo puts the place, its child places and its
chats back. When the toast leaves on its own, the place stays deleted and the
chats stay where they moved.
