# Desktop draft places and Home views

## Why is there an arrow place pill while I type in the desktop composer?

The desktop can show “→ Place” beside the model chip before the first reply. It asks for a suggestion after 300 milliseconds without typing. A missing answer, a failed request or a request taking longer than 20 milliseconds draws nothing. Editing immediately hides the previous suggestion.

Choose the pill to change the suggested place. The choice stays in this view until the first settled reply; then it is filed as your change with Undo. Choosing or typing alone does not file anything. Closing the view or reloading before that reply loses the local choice. Automatic filing remains the engine's decision. A chat started on a place's Home keeps that Home's explicit place and has no suggestion pill.

## What does also in mean on a desktop Home chat row?

“also in” marks a chat shown here through a shortcut membership. When the engine supplies its primary place name, the row says “also in <primary place>”. Primary memberships and unknown memberships show no mark.

## How do I filter the chats with views chips on a desktop place Home?

Views are named slices of the place's chats, never new places. Press a view chip to filter the chat rows already loaded on Home to that view. Press the selected chip again to show all chats. The selected chip has a soft fill. A refreshed Home reading clears the filter. If the engine supplies no views, the chips are absent.

## How do I steer a desktop place with Organize this by?

On a connected place Home, type in “Organize this by…” and press Enter or Apply. The sentence applies to this place. A successful reply clears the field and rereads its views. An unrecognised sentence stays in the field and says “I did not understand that; try 'by month' or 'by project'”. A request failure shows its error beside the field. Offline Homes have no steer field.
