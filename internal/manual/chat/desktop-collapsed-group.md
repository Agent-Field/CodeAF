# Collapsed tab groups on the desktop

## Where is my collapsed group when I am in another tab

A collapsed group stays in the tab strip as a pill, the group name and the number of tabs in it, such as "Release 2". That pill stays when the tab you are looking at is outside the group. The tabs inside are hidden until you open the group. Arrow keys and the number shortcuts skip those hidden tabs.

## How do I open a collapsed group of desktop tabs

Click the pill, or press Enter or Space while it is focused. The group opens and its tabs come back into the strip. The tab you were already in stays selected. Press the pill again to collapse it.

## The group pill shows Needs you when a hidden tab needs you

When a tab inside a collapsed group needs you, the pill carries a small amber dot and a screen reader hears "Needs you". The dot is the only colour. Opening the group shows that same dot on the tab that needs you, and the pill itself drops the dot. Running work inside the group stays quiet: no count and no spinner on the pill or on the tab.
