# Discussions on the place home

## Where does a running discussion show on the place home

A discussion that is still running, or paused because you started typing, is
on that place's home with the other work that is still going. The row names
the two places the way the chat is titled, for example "Marketing with
Software", and names the topic under it. A discussion that has decided, or
that stopped because it hit its limit, is not on that list. It is a chat in
History. A parent place lists a discussion when either place sits in it or
under it. The same chat is not also listed as a task.

## What does 2 of 6 turns mean on the place home

The discussion row says how many turns have been taken and the cap, which is
6. Two turns taken reads as 2 of 6 turns. Zero turns taken is still shown, as
0 of 6 turns. A message you send into the discussion does not count as a
turn. The replies it brings do. A discussion is also capped at $0.25.

## Why is closed work still listed on the place home

Closing the chat does not take work off the home while that work is still
running. The home lists a task whose window still has the chat, and it lists
the same kind of work after the window has let go. A chat that is waiting for
you is on that list too, under the reason it is waiting, such as "Publish the
launch post?". A chat with nothing running and nothing waiting is left off.
When nothing is live, the list is empty.

## Where do council chats appear in history

A council chat appears in History as soon as the discussion exists, including
before anyone has typed in it. The title is the two place names with "with"
between them, for example "Marketing with Software". Deleting that chat takes
it out of History. It is an ordinary chat filed in both places.

## How do I steer a discussion that is still going

Send a message in the discussion. It keeps going, and it answers you first.
Your message does not count as one of the 6 turns. A discussion that has
already decided or been escalated cannot be steered. A place home that has no
discussions still lists a task that is running.

## What does the decided count on a place home mean

The home can say how many discussions filed in this place, or in a place
under it, have decided. When none have, it says nothing about that. A
discussion that was escalated is not part of that count. The count is not a
log of decisions the product made on its own.
