# Desktop Home pinning

## Why can't I close the first place Home tab with Command W

A place's Home stays first in its desktop tab strip, with the place's swatch and name. It has no close button. Command W on macOS (Control W elsewhere) leaves it open. Closing another tab can return you to Home. Home cannot be unpinned, moved, grouped or split.

## Is the All places tab also pinned or can I close it

“All places” uses the root Home page, but is an ordinary closable tab in the place where you opened it. Its close button and Command W (Control W elsewhere) close that view. They do not delete any places or stop their work. Command Shift P (Control Shift P elsewhere) opens a fresh All places tab each time. A plain click on the rail’s All places row focuses an existing one when available. Switching places keeps each place's own saved tab set.

## Can I use arrow keys to reach the desktop Home tab

Focus a tab in the desktop strip. Left and right arrows move through its visible tabs, including Home. Home and End reach the first and last visible tabs. With the rail hidden, clicking Home opens the place switcher; with the rail showing, it focuses the place's Home page.
