# Desktop Home pinning

## Why can't I close the first place Home tab with Command W

A place's Home is no longer pinned first. It is an ordinary tab with the place's swatch and name, opened after the active tab when you click the place in the rail, and it has the usual close button and Command W (Control W elsewhere). Closing it closes only that tab: the place, its chats and its rail row stay.

## Is the All places tab also pinned or can I close it

“All places” uses the root Home page, but is an ordinary closable tab in the place where you opened it. Its close button and Command W (Control W elsewhere) close that view. They do not delete any places or stop their work. Command Shift P (Control Shift P elsewhere) opens a fresh All places tab each time. A plain click on the rail’s All places row focuses an existing one when available. Going to a place never changes the tab set.

## Can I use arrow keys to reach the desktop Home tab

Focus a tab in the desktop strip. Left and right arrows move through its visible tabs, including Home tabs. Home and End reach the first and last visible tabs. Clicking a Home tab focuses that place's Home page.
