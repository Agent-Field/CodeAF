# Desktop appearance settings

## Switch the desktop app between light and dark theme

Settings has an Appearance section with one theme choice: System appearance, Light appearance or Dark appearance. The choice applies at once, with no Save button, and is kept when the app is reloaded or reopened. System follows the operating system's light or dark setting.

## Turn off animations or reduce motion in the desktop app

There is no reduce motion control. Under the theme choice, Settings says "Reduce motion follows your system setting." The desktop app reads the operating system's reduce motion preference and cannot be told otherwise from Settings.
