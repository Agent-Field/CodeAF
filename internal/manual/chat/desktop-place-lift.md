# Places that are not one subject

## Why did organize take these places out from under the place they were in

Organize can offer to put places on their own when the place above them is not really their parent. That happens when the places are not about one thing. Each one then stands at the top, beside the place that used to hold it. The chats that were filed on that place stay there. The places that were inside one of the separated places stay inside it, one step higher than they were.

The offer says: "These places are not about one thing, so each one stands on its own." Nothing moves until you accept it.

## Two projects were filed under one place and they are not the same project

A place can end up holding two projects that do not belong together. Organize treats that parent as not a parent. Each project is offered a place of its own at the top. A project that shares a subject with the other one stays where it is. So do places that clearly sit together. A place with nothing to compare against stays where it is too: no position is not a reason to separate it.

## Why did these places stay inside the one above them when the top is already full

The top holds at most 9 places side by side. When putting the places on their own would pass that, they stay inside the place they are in. The offer is still made, and it says they stay inside this one for now: "These places are not about one thing. Putting each one on its own would leave more than 9 places side by side, so they stay inside this one for now." Accepting it is still your choice. Until you do, the places stay nested.
