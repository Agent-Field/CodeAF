# Place tint in the menu

## How do I change a place's colour in the desktop menu

Right-click the place and find Tint. Five squares sit on the next line: Tide, Rose, Sage, Sand and Iris, in that order. The place's current colour has a ring around it. Left and right move the ring, and the row wraps. Enter sets that colour and closes the menu. Clicking a square does the same. Up and down leave the squares for the other commands in the menu.

## Why is graphite missing from the tint menu

Graphite means no colour was chosen. The menu never offers it. A place that has no colour of its own opens with no ring, and Enter does nothing until you move onto a square.

## What do the arrow keys do on the tint squares

Left and right move between the five squares. Up and down move to the other rows of the menu. Enter picks the square the ring is on.
