# Desktop notifications

## Which desktop questions send a system notification?

While codeaf is in the background, a blocking question or a failed task sends one system notification per item. Questions that only offer a suggestion without holding up a turn or task stay quiet. Already decided questions never notify. Notifications share a group named for the conversation's place; unplaced work belongs to Now. While the app is focused, new items stay quiet.

## What happens when I click a desktop notification?

Clicking a question notification starts Next up at that exact item and opens its conversation with the question in front. The header shows your position in the walk. Answering or withdrawing that question advances to the next waiting question. A question already answered opens only its conversation, without starting a walk. A failed-task notification opens its conversation.

## Why does the desktop dock badge include my current conversation?

The dock or launcher badge counts all questions needing you, including the current conversation and questions that do not block work. Failures do not add to that count. Zero clears the badge. Operating-system permissions can silence notifications; browser previews do not post system notifications.
