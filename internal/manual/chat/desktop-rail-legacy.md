# Desktop rail

## Find anything is not on the desktop rail

The desktop rail has no Find anything row and no address field. Command-K (Control-K on Linux) opens the New-tab field. Under the toggle the list is Now, then the open rows, All places, and Settings at the bottom.

## How do I open Settings from the desktop sidebar?

Choose the Settings row at the bottom of the desktop rail. That opens the Settings tab, the same tab Command-comma opens (Control-comma on Linux). The row shows that chord in grey. Right-clicking the row opens nothing. While the rail is hidden, Command-comma still opens Settings.

## Where is the theme on the desktop rail?

The desktop rail has no theme control. Open Settings and use Appearance. The control is named Theme, and the choices are System appearance, Light appearance and Dark appearance. Reduce motion follows your system setting.

## Activity is not on the desktop rail

The desktop rail does not list Activity. It lists Now, the open rows, All places, and Settings.

## Why does the desktop rail show a Design system row?

A development build draws a Design system row just above Settings, for the component specimens. The app you install does not draw that row.
