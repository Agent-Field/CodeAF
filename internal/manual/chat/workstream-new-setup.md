# A new workstream — Afi asks what it is for, then proposes a card you create

## new workstream — how do I create a workstream, what happens when I press +

Pressing **+** beside Work (or File › New Workstream, or From scratch on the Work home) opens a screen called "New
workstream". It is only a conversation with Afi, with no tabs yet, and **nothing exists and nobody is told until you
press Create workstream**. Afi's first line is:

"What is this workstream for? A sentence is enough."

You answer in your own words in the box at the bottom. Pressing + again opens the same screen with what is already on
it; there is one new-workstream screen per window, and closing the window forgets it. Nothing is left behind, so there
is no empty "New workstream" to clean up.

## Afi's questions when setting up — why did Afi ask me something, how many questions

Afi proposes as soon as it can. When what you said leaves something it cannot guess and that changes the setup (who
decides, a date), it asks at most two short questions, in one message, with its best-guess card already under them: answer
and it changes the card, or change the card yourself. It never asks about rules, spending, names or
files: it drafts sensible ones and you change them on the card. Afi may suggest only people already in the
organization, and only Library documents you can open.

## the workstream card — what is on it, editing the setup before it exists

Afi answers "Here is how I would set it up. Change anything, then create it." and the card appears in the
conversation. Every line is a value you change in place:

- **Name**, and **For**: what it is for, with the date if there is one.
- **People** (an organization only): you, and the people and teams Afi named. A name nobody in the organization
  answers to stays dim, "not in <organization>", and is left out. **Add people or a team** adds more.
- **Inside**: "Not inside another workstream", or a top-level one; inside, it takes that one's people.
- **First tasks**: up to seven, each ticked. Each says who does it and for whom: "Afi for you", "Sam", "You · due
  Nov 20". Work that comes round says so, "Afi for you · every Friday", and becomes a standing order. Untick a task to
  leave it out; rename, remove or add tasks.
- **Files**: "Reads Pricing and Pilot template from the Library." (a Personal workstream only), and the Brief, a
  document written from the card.
- **Rules**: plain lines such as "Afi asks before anything is sent to Northwind". Who decides, who answers incoming
  work, the fallback and Start without asking show only when Afi set them.

**Tell Afi what to change** in the box ("make Sam the owner of the weekly report") and Afi redraws the whole card,
starting from your changes.

## Create workstream — what Create does, who is told

Beside **Create workstream** the card says who is told, for example "Sam is told when you create it." Anyone a
ticked task is given to joins as one of its people and is told too. Personal workstreams tell nobody. Create makes everything at once: the workstream with its name, what it is for, where it sits
and its people; the standing orders; the rules; each rule line as a decision; the Brief in Files; the ticked tasks
together as the group "First plan", each after what it waits on; and last, in an organization, the workstream handed
to who decides. A toast says "<name> is created" and the screen opens the workstream’s Overview. If a part cannot be made the card stays, names it ("Couldn't create the Brief ·
…"), and **Create the rest** does only what is left, never a second workstream.

## Set up by hand — creating a workstream without Afi, no model

**Set up by hand**, at the top right of the screen, opens the same card empty, with no conversation and no model asked.
Create workstream waits until it has a name. When this computer has no model for Afi, the screen says so and opens the
empty card at once.

## making a workstream from a chat — Make workstream

Make workstream in a chat opens the same screen; Afi's first line says it will bring the chat along, and "Set up by
hand" starts the card with the chat's name. On Create the chat comes into the new workstream's Chats. Making a
workstream from a document is not there yet.
