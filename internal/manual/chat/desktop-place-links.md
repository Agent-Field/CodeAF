# Also in, and views of a place

## Why does a chat say also in this place

A chat has one home. When a second place fits it almost as well, that place is marked on the chat as also in. Organize now records the line "Also in this place." There are at most two of those. A place that is merely close, but not that close, is not marked, and a chat that fits nowhere is not marked either.

## How many other places can one chat also be in

At most two. The place that fits best is not marked. The marks are the next places, closest first, and a third place that fits just as well is left off.

## What is a view of this place by month

A view is a way of looking at the chats already in a place. It is not another place, and it does not file the chats anywhere else. A top-level place with at least 15 chats gets one view named "by month". The plan says "A view of this place by month." The months come from the dates the chats already have. A chat with no date is not given one. A place under another place does not get its own by-month view from the size of its list; the top-level place's view covers the chats under it.

Asking to organise a place by month adds that same view even when the place has fewer than 15 chats. The line is still "A view of this place by month."

## Is a view of these chats its own place

No. When a group of chats is one kind of activity rather than a place you would open, it stays a view on the place above it. The plan says "A view of these chats, not a place." The view is named from a word the chats themselves use. It never appears in the place list as a place of its own, and closing or opening places does not create one from a view.

## What does by project show on a place

When a place is organised by project, each project the place was given becomes a view, and the view lists the chats that name that project. A project no chat names is not shown. The plan says "A view of this place by project." A folder axis says "A view of this place by folder." A kind-of-work axis says "A view of this place by kind of work." An axis with no values adds no view.
