# Desktop iteration two

## Where did the desktop Inbox go and how do I open Next up?

Next up replaces the desktop Inbox tab and rail row. The window frame pill counts
questions in other conversations: “N need you elsewhere”. Click it or press ⌘J
(Ctrl+J on Linux and Windows) to visit the actual conversations with their trays
open. The current chat keeps its own “needs you here” count. No pending questions
elsewhere means no pill. Closed work that is still running is under Live on its
place Home. Skip moves a question to the back without answering; the walk ends
with “You're clear”. Accept suggestions covers only reversible, non-costly
questions with suggestions, with Undo before the delayed answers are sent.

## How do I go back after Next up or go up a level on desktop Home?

⌘[ and ⌘] move back and forward through foreground focus history, restoring the
place, tab, scroll and draft. Linux and Windows use Alt+Left and Alt+Right.
Home goes up a level with ⌘↑ (Ctrl+Up), outside a text field. In a chat that chord
moves to the previous message instead. Escape leaves Next up. An unrequested
jump offers a temporary “Back to …” chip; a drill inside a tab has a parent chip.
⌘I no longer opens Inbox.

## When do desktop places decide automatically instead of asking me?

A filed chat can let its place answer before a question is shown. The default
sureness threshold is 90 percent. Irreversible actions, always-ask settings and
policy refusals still ask you. New kinds learn first: the proposed answer is
already selected where the question appears, with its measured reason and
sureness when known. Only 18 agreements among the last 20 personal answers
qualify a kind to decide. Fewer than 20 answers never qualifies. Automatic
answers do not teach agreement. Overturn sends only that kind back to learning.
A deciding place below threshold can pass a question to parents within three
hops, then to you. Judgements, confirmations, landing and clarification questions
remain personal under the current session policy.

## What are automatic decision receipts and can I overturn them?

An automatic answer leaves “Allowed automatically by … · Why?” under the
permission, or “Decided” for another question. Why? reveals recorded facts;
“Did N things” expands grouped actions. The place log keeps decisions newest
first, including overturned ones. The desktop transcript currently shows the
explanation but does not wire Overturn or the next-time controls there. The
confirmation component and engine route are separate: a missing reversal door
refuses instead of claiming success. Dependent tasks require a separate choice
to notify or pause; overturn never silently changes them.

A plan reaching another chat, task or place waits for Go, Edit or Cancel.
Cancelled plans run nothing, repeated Go runs once, and deleted targets get a
skipped receipt. Undo appears only where the engine can reverse that step.

## What does a desktop place know and where does remember save it?

“What [place] knows” combines knowledge lines with their real sources. Home
shows three and All expands them. Remember in a filed chat saves a place line
with Undo; explicit user, project and environment scopes keep general memory.
Using draws the live knowledge through the same resolver as the engine.
Newer replacements win and the old line is struck through for seven days.
Same-day personal conflicts ask once; sixty unused days makes “still true?” due.
The bridge needs an explicitly named replacement and does not detect arbitrary
prose contradictions. Home edit controls require real write callbacks; absent
callbacks mean absent actions. Unknown counts, dates and confidence stay blank.

## Why is there a desktop discussion called Marketing with Software?

Two places can discuss one topic in an ordinary chat filed in both, with messages
labelled by place. Typing pauses it until you send and steer it. A settled chat
shows its recorded decision; missing cost or outcome is never invented.
The discussion runner caps at six place turns and $0.25, then escalates. The
same pair/topic cannot reopen while running or paused, or before an hour from
its opening after it ends. The council view needs a connected runner and
escalation handler; rendering it alone does not start those services.
