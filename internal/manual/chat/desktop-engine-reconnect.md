# Reconnecting to the engine

## Reconnecting to the engine under the conversation header

When the engine stops responding, one muted line appears under the conversation header. It reads "Reconnecting to the engine…". The app keeps trying, and the pause between tries grows. The composer stays editable and the draft stays where you left it. The line is not red.

## Can't reach the engine after 30 seconds, and Retry

After 30 seconds with no response the line reads "Can't reach the engine" and shows Retry. Retry is a quiet button. Pressing it tries once more. While that try is in progress, the line reads "Reconnecting to the engine…" again. If the engine is still out of reach, "Can't reach the engine" and Retry come back. Nothing turns red.

## The engine line under the header disappears when it reconnects

When the engine is back, the line under the header goes away. The conversation does not say "codeaf engine is not running".
