# Deleting chats in desktop History

## How do I delete a chat from History

In History, right-click an archived chat and choose Delete…. The row itself
is replaced by a line that names the count: "Delete 1 chat?". Cancel is the
quiet button. Delete is the danger button. Escape, or Cancel, puts the row
back. History does not open a dialog.

Only an archived chat can be deleted. A chat that is still open is refused,
and Delete… is not in its menu. Archive it first.

## Delete 14 chats

Shift-click selects the run of rows from your last plain click to the row you
click. Delete… on one of those rows replaces the first archived row in the
run with "Delete 14 chats?" when fourteen archived chats are selected. Chats
in the run that are not archived are left out of the count. Delete… on a row
outside the run asks only about that row.

## Undo deleted chats

After Delete, a toast says "1 chat deleted" or "14 chats deleted" and offers
Undo for 10 seconds. Undo puts those chats back into History. When the toast
leaves on its own, the chats stay deleted.
