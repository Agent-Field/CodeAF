# Desktop shell questions

## Where does the Inbox tab sit next to Home?

The place's Home tab is the first tab in the strip, and it stays pinned. If an Inbox tab is open, it is the next pinned tab, after Home. It is never in front of Home. Closing a tab with the mouse's middle button does not close Home or any other pinned tab.

## What does middle-click do on a desktop tab?

Middle-click closes that tab, the same way the close mark does. The work keeps running. A pinned tab, including the Inbox, stays open. A place's Home stays open. On a split, the middle-click closes the whole split, every pane of it.

## Why doesn't right-click on Inbox or Now open a menu?

Inbox and Now in the side list have no menu. Right-click does nothing there. A place's own row still has its menu.

## How many notices sit at the bottom of the desktop window?

One. A newer notice replaces the one already there. It stays while the pointer is on it or while it has the keyboard, and Escape dismisses it. A notice that something failed is announced as an alert. An ordinary notice is a status.
