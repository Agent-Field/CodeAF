# Desktop council chats between two places

A desktop council is the chat the desktop app opens when two places discuss one topic.

## Why is there a chat called Marketing with Software

When two places discuss one topic, the desktop app opens an ordinary chat and titles it
with the two place names and the word "with" between them, for example
"Marketing with Software". The title uses the names in the order the discussion
was opened. It is filed in both places. It is not a separate kind of
conversation: you read it and send messages in it the same way as any other chat.

## Why two places cannot reopen the same topic for an hour

The same two places cannot open another chat on that topic while the first is
still running or paused. Saying the places in the other order is the same pair.
A question that differs only by capitals or spacing is the same topic. After
the discussion has ended, they cannot reopen it until an hour after the first
one opened. A different topic, or a different place, can open immediately.

## What limits a discussion between two places

Each discussion is limited to 6 turns and $0.25. It is running, paused,
decided, or escalated. A decided discussion keeps its outcome, such as
"promise it for v2.4.1". An escalated discussion has ended because it hit a
limit. Until it ends, it has no outcome. Nobody has to have typed in the chat
for it to exist; the title is already the two place names.
