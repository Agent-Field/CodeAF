# Agents in an organization — your own agents in My space, sharing one into a workstream, and the organization's list

## Making my own agent in an organization — My space › Agents

Inside an organization window, My space has an **Agents** tab. **New agent** asks for a name and instructions: what the agent does and how ("Write short, plain launch copy; never more than 120 words"). It is yours alone. Nobody else in the organization is told it exists, the operator included, until you share it. It runs on the organization's server with the organization's key, and what it spends counts against your My space limit.

A name is at most 60 characters and cannot be "Afi", who is already in every workstream. Instructions are at most 8000 bytes. You can keep 100 agents. **Edit** changes the name or the instructions, and **Delete** removes the agent and takes it off every workstream it was shared into. Only you can change or delete your agent, even after it is shared or on the organization's list.

Your Personal agents never appear in an organization window, and these never appear in Personal.

## Chatting with one of my agents — a chat that is not Afi

**Chat** on an agent's row starts a chat with it in My space. The chat answers as that agent, following its instructions, and is private to you like every chat in My space. The chat's row shows the agent's name. Its other rules stay the same: it reads and writes your space, and it cannot make tasks there.

In a workstream you can chat with any agent that workstream lists (below). If the agent is taken off the workstream or deleted, its chats stay readable but take no more messages: "Copywriter is no longer one this workstream lists, so this chat takes no more messages; start a new chat".

## Sharing an agent into a workstream — People and rules › Agents

**Share to workstream…** on your agent's row adds it to a workstream you can edit. The workstream gets a link, not a copy: everyone in the workstream sees it by name under People and rules › Agents, as "shared by Priya from her space", and can chat with it. Only you change it. Sharing does not show the agent to people outside that workstream.

The Agents part of a workstream's People and rules lists Afi first, then each agent the workstream may use. Anyone who can edit the workstream can add one (**Add an agent**: from your own, or from the organization's list) or take one off. You can always take your own agent off a workstream. A workstream lists 50 agents at most, and My space takes none: your agents are already there.

These are the agents a task in that workstream may be given to. Ask Afi ("give the launch post to Copywriter") and it makes the task with that agent as its doer; when the task starts, the organization's server runs it as that agent, with the agent's instructions and the task's request, never a private chat. An agent the workstream has not added is refused, and one taken off the workstream, or deleted, stops being offered at once. No screen offers an agent as a doer: the Done by choices are Afi, your chats, you and the workstream's people.

## The organization's list of agents — asking the operator to approve an agent for everyone

**Ask for the organization's list** on your agent's row sends it to the operator. Until they decide, it reads "Waiting for the operator", and still nobody else sees it. The operator reads its name and instructions in Settings › Organizations under "Agents waiting for you", and chooses **Approve** or **Decline**. Approved, it is on the organization's list: everyone in the organization sees it, can chat with it in their space, and can add it to a workstream they edit. An operator's own agent goes on the list without waiting.

**Take off the list** (yours, or the operator's) removes it. It then leaves every workstream someone else added it to, and stays in the ones you shared it into yourself.

## What the operator sees of my agents

Nothing, until you ask for the organization's list. An agent you keep to yourself counts as one of the things in your space in the operator's totals ("Priya · $0.84 this month · 5 things"). Its name, its instructions and its chats are never shown. The organization's activity says only that an agent was asked for, approved, declined, added or removed, never which.

When the operator removes someone from the organization, their agents go to the heir with the rest of their space. Workstreams they shared an agent into keep it, now shared by the heir.

## What agents in an organization cannot do yet

There are no crews in an organization: an agent works alone. Only Afi gives a task to a named agent, and only to one on the workstream's Agents list. Agents cannot be moved into a workstream (only shared). An agent cannot be given tools beyond those a chat in that place has.
