# Moving a tab group in the desktop strip

## Move a whole group — drag the group label, or keyboard shortcut Alt+Shift+arrows to move a tab group left or right

Drag a group's label (the name at the start of the group) and drop it before or after any tab or other group in the tab strip; the whole group moves, and its tabs keep their order. Dropped on a tab inside another group, it lands beside that whole group, never inside it, because groups do not nest.

With the label focused, Alt+Shift+← or Alt+Shift+→ moves the group one slot, past the next loose tab or whole group. The strip announces it for screen readers: "Moved group Release to position 3 of 4". At either end of the strip nothing happens.

## Pinned tabs and groups — what a group move never does

Pinned tabs stay first and belong to no group. Dropping a group before a pinned tab puts it just after the last pinned tab, and a pinned tab never joins a group by a group being moved. Moving a group to where it already stands changes nothing. Cmd+Z takes a group move back.
