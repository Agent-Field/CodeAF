# Desktop keyboard tab reorder

## How do I move desktop tabs left or right with the keyboard

Focus a tab in the desktop tab strip and press Alt+Shift+Left or
Alt+Shift+Right. It moves one position within its section: pinned tabs,
loose tabs, or the members of its own group. The place Home stays fixed.
The shortcut keeps focus on the same tab, including the same segment of a
split tab. It keeps the active tab and grouping picks unchanged.

## What does Moved to position N of M mean when reordering desktop tabs

After a keyboard move, a polite screen-reader announcement says
“Moved to position N of M”, counting the movable tabs in that section.
At either end the tab stays put; the shortcut does not wrap, cross into
another section, or announce a move that did not happen.
