# Work home and the Overview tab

## Work home — all your workstreams, Waiting on you, Your work

Work opens the page across the workstreams you belong to. It never includes a workstream merely because you are an organization operator. Personal and an organization have separate Work pages.

“Waiting on you” brings together your open Asks. Four cards are shown with their answer buttons; more questions have an “Open” row. Answering uses the task's own answer, so the question also leaves Overview and the Inbox. “Later” puts that question away until tomorrow at 09:00. It does not answer it.

“Your work” shows a row per top-level workstream, including the progress of its children. A row opens its Overview. “Afi and your chats are doing” lists your delegated work. “Yours to do this week” shows your own person tasks due within seven days; the checkbox completes the task. “You are waiting on” shows unfinished work you requested that someone else owns. A section with no items is absent.

## Overview first — purpose, Running, Done today, results

A workstream opens on “Overview”, before “Tasks”, “Chats” and “Files”. The purpose is the same field as the workstream's goal. “Edit purpose” saves it for the workstream and its Afi.

Overview shows at most two Asks, four Running rows and two results from today. With fewer than four running tasks it opens their last three work steps. A larger list uses one row per piece of work, grouping tasks under their shared heading. “All tasks” opens Tasks. Empty sections and side blocks are absent.

A result previews the first four lines, up to 600 characters, of the actual result document in Files. An upload or link has no invented text preview. “Send back” opens that task's existing Send back composer: the same task action and doer rules apply.

“Asked of another workstream” shows a request's title, owner, destination and state. It does not reveal the destination's other tasks or private conversations.

## Afi has a plan — ticks and Start N

A batch still waiting in Up next appears as a plan on Overview. A tick selects a task that its doer can start now. “Start N” counts the selected runnable tasks only. Tasks waiting on unfinished work, person tasks and unconnected factory jobs do not pretend to be running. Unticking a task leaves it untouched when Start is pressed.

## Handed to you and Archived

“Handed to you” lists tasks someone handed you. Opening one removes that handoff from this computer's Work page for your account; the task remains in its workstream.

The Work rail nests children under their parent. “Archived · N” sits below the live list and opens the archive. A filtered or archived-only list does not say “No workstreams yet”.
