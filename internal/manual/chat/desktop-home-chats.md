# Desktop Home chats

## What is the digest under a chat on desktop Home?

The Chats section on a place's Home lists chats filed directly in that place.
Each row shows its title, an available engine-written recap line, and a short
relative time when the engine supplies a timestamp. A chat waiting for you
shows the engine's waiting reason in place of its recap. A missing recap or
time draws nothing. Home does not call a model to write a digest.

Running chats have a running dot; settled chats have a message glyph.

## Does clicking a chat on desktop Home open another tab?

Click a chat in the Chats section to open it in the current place's tab strip.
If its saved conversation already has an open tab or pane there, that tab or
pane receives focus. A closed tab is reopened with its retained view state.
If the engine cannot supply the saved conversation's location, Home shows
“This chat cannot be opened here: the engine did not say where it is saved.”
and opens no tab.
