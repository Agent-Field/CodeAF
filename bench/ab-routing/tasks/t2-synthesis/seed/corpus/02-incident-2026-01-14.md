# Incident report — 2026-01-14 — token refresh storm

**Affected service:** svc-auth
**Severity:** SEV-1
**Status:** closed, actions tracked in PLAT-4471

## Summary

A configuration push at 02:15 UTC shortened the access-token lifetime from
sixty minutes to sixty seconds. Every client began refreshing once a minute.
The refresh path publishes an audit message per refresh, and the resulting
volume saturated the service's queue client.

## Timeline

All times UTC.

- 02:15 — configuration pushed.
- 02:41 — first alert; publish latency past the page threshold.
- 02:52 — on-call engaged; cause not yet identified.
- 03:20 — the configuration push is identified as the trigger.
- 03:34 — rollback begins.
- 04:05 — rollback complete; refresh rate returns to baseline.
- 05:40 — the audit backlog finishes draining and error rates return to
  normal. **This is the end of customer impact.**

## Customer impact

Login and token refresh were degraded or failing for a substantial share of
requests. **Customer impact lasted 3 hours and 40 minutes**, measured from the
first alert at 02:41 to the backlog clearing at 05:40.

We are deliberately measuring impact from first alert rather than from the
configuration push: the shortened lifetime was harmless until enough clients
had rotated onto it.

## Follow-up

- Bound token-lifetime changes to a ratchet that cannot shorten by more than
  4x in one push. *(done)*
- Sample the refresh audit stream rather than publishing every event. *(open)*
