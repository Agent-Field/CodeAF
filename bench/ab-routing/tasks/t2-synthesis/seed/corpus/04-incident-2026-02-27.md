# Incident report — 2026-02-27 — certificate expiry on the identity mesh

**Affected service:** svc-auth
**Severity:** SEV-1
**Status:** closed, actions tracked in PLAT-4602

## Summary

The internal mesh certificate used between svc-auth and its session store
expired. Renewal had been automated in 2024 and the automation had been
silently failing since a runner image change in November.

## Timeline

- **2026-02-27 22:40 UTC — certificate expires; customer impact begins.**
  New session creation fails immediately; existing sessions continue to work
  until they need to touch the session store.
- 2026-02-27 22:44 UTC — paged.
- 2026-02-27 23:30 UTC — expiry identified as the cause.
- 2026-02-28 00:10 UTC — a certificate is issued by hand.
- 2026-02-28 00:55 UTC — rollout to the last mesh node completes.
- **2026-02-28 01:25 UTC — session creation success rate returns to baseline;
  end of customer impact.**

Note that this incident crosses midnight UTC. It is filed under its start date.

## Customer impact

New logins failed outright for the duration. Sessions established before the
expiry degraded gradually as they aged out.

## Follow-up

- Alert on certificate expiry at 30, 14 and 3 days. *(done)*
- Fail the renewal job loudly instead of exiting zero on error. *(done)*
- Audit every other automation that was pinned to the old runner image.
  *(open)*
