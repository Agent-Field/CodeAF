# Incident report — 2026-02-03 — index rebuild wedged

**Affected service:** svc-search
**Severity:** SEV-2
**Status:** closed

## Summary

A nightly index rebuild acquired a lock it never released. Query traffic
continued to be served from the stale index; writes queued behind the lock.
Search results were up to eleven hours out of date by the time the lock was
cleared.

## Timeline

All times UTC.

- 03:00 — nightly rebuild starts as scheduled.
- 03:06 — rebuild acquires the write lock.
- 14:07 — **customer impact begins**: the staleness alert fires and merchants
  begin reporting that newly listed items are not findable.
- 14:30 — on-call engaged.
- 15:11 — the wedged rebuild is identified.
- 15:52 — the lock is force-released, the rebuild reruns, and the index catches
  up. **End of customer impact.**

Impact is measured from the staleness alert at 14:07, not from the lock being
taken at 03:06: an index that is a few hours stale is within the product's
stated freshness promise and nobody was affected until it was not.

## Notes from the write-up

svc-search publishes its rebuild-progress events through **pulsar-edge**, so
the queue itself was never implicated and we did not investigate it further.

## Follow-up

- Lease the rebuild lock with a TTL rather than holding it. *(done)*
- Alert on rebuild duration, not only on index staleness. *(done)*
