6. BREAK-EVEN: at what task cost does the probe pay for itself?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Probe cost is ~fixed (7 short calls, output capped at 96-256 tokens); attempt cost
scales with the size of the job. Sweep k = attempt-cost multiplier (k=1 is measured).

'$ per success' is the wrong lens on its own: it never charges for a failure, so
always-cheap wins by simply failing a third of the time for free. The decision-relevant
quantity is total expected cost INCLUDING a downstream cost F for each unsolved task
(a failed agent step costs a retry loop, a human, or a bad artifact). Sweep F.
