The detector was right, on time, and in the right words, and nothing acted on it.
After the standstill sentence was printed, one measured run paid two delivery-gate
calls of ~114K tokens whose refusal described work that did not exist, ran a full
twenty-turn repair round of the same leaf against that refusal, re-judged it, said
the sentence again, and resumed the same leaf a third time — every one of those
over a tree that was byte-for-byte the tree the leaf had started from. The law is
that once the harness has concluded nothing is changing, it stops spending on that
job. The gate and the repair are stopped by a measurement of the tree; the resume
is stopped by the verdict itself, which now travels on the result rather than only
into a journal nobody was reading.
