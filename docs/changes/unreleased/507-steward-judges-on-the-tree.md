Every reading this side of the engine took of "is it done" was a reading of what
the session SAID. The one place that looked at the work — the goal owner's own
decision — looked at it underneath a sidecar's opinion of the transcript, ran a
source file as though it were a build, and had no floor of its own, so a finished
ask could be pushed on until the hours ran out. The order of the decision is now
an order over evidence, a check is a command or it is not a check, and a run that
is saying the same thing twice stops and says which thing.
