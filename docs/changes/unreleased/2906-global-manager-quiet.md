invalidates:
  - "A member's finished, failed or asking event started its manager's turn in every team, the team that holds every team included, where only an idle (stopped) event did not. In that team no member's finished event starts the global manager's turn any longer (teamEventWakesManagerOf): of a member's events there only failed and asking do, and a team_post to the manager, a decision packet a member raises and a conflict it declares wake the global manager as before. A global manager nobody has open is likewise no longer opened by a finished event there. The rule is that whole team's: it covers a top-level team's manager, a manager that was replaced or whose team closed and kept its seat there, and a conversation the person put there, since the global manager lists and directs only the current top-level managers (managedView). Every other team is unchanged."
  - "team_send answered every manager \"Replies and endings wake you when they arrive, so there is no need to wait or poll.\" In the team that holds every team it now answers \"Their replies, questions and failures wake you when they arrive, so there is no need to wait or poll. A top-level team's manager answers a directive with team_post: its turn finishing or being stopped does not wake you, and team_status says where it stands.\" The answer in every other team is as it was."
  - "The global manager's team note said \"Members' replies to you, and their finishing, failing or asking, come back to you and start your turn when you are idle\". For the global manager it now says \"Members' replies to you, and their failing or asking, come back to you\" and ends \"A member's turn finishing or being stopped does not start yours.\", and its scope adds \"A manager answers a directive of yours, and the brief you started it on, with team_post, since its turn finishing does not start yours; team_status says where each of them stands. The person talks to those managers directly, and what the person asked one of them is that manager's work already: never send a manager the person's own request to it as a directive of yours.\" Both are in the global manager's role note, which it is handed when that note's text changes."
  - "A top-level manager's note about its seat in the team that holds every team ended \"you report to it with team_post to the manager.\" It now adds \"Your turn ending tells it nothing: answer each of its directives, and the brief it started you on if it started you, with team_post to the manager. Apart from those answers, post only what it should act on or tell the person.\" The rest of that note is as it was. Every member of that team was given that note, \"because you manage a top-level team\" included; a member there that manages no top-level team now is given the note a member of any team is given."
  - "The first turn of a team's new manager, on the brief team_start of kind team started it with, woke the manager that started it when it finished, and team_start answered every manager that the new conversation \"makes itself the manager of\" the team \"and reports to you.\" A team the global manager starts no longer wakes it that way, and team_start answers the global manager that the new manager \"reports to you with team_post: its turn finishing on your brief does not start yours, and team_status says where it stands.\" The answer to every other manager is as it was."
---

The global manager's members are the managers of whole teams, each a conversation
the person talks to, and every answer one of them gave ended a turn. Measured in
the desktop app, one afternoon of that woke the global manager 22 times for
803,496 input tokens, each answered "[no change]", and one earlier wake had it
send the person's own request back to a manager as a directive.
