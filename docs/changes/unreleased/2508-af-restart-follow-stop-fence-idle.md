---
kind: fixed
title: AgentField runs survive a restart and Stop, fenced checks write receipts, the idle engine stays idle
pr: 2508
surface: [factory, engine]
invalidates:
  - "Was true (#2506 and before): a job waiting in Needs you on its AgentField run's approval lost its hold on an engine restart and was never queued again (expiry queued only running jobs); approving it with no runner holding it turned it running with no lease, and no runner ever took it, so it read \"running\" while its run finished at the control plane and opened its pull request (jobs 71–73 of the third live run). Is true now: a job whose AgentField run is live, with no hold, is offered to a runner while it is running, queued, or waiting on that run's own approval (lease.owesFollow); the runner follows the same run, carries any decision it owes, and the run's end lands on the job. No retry is spent and the question stays up while it is re-followed."
  - "Was true: a Stop pressed while no runner held an AgentField job only wrote the job; the run went on and could open a pull request after the Stop. Is true now: such a stopped job is offered to the next runner, held to talk only (lease.owesStop), and that runner cancels the whole run on the control plane and writes its end on the job (worker.Options.CarryStop, afRemote.CarryStop). Once the run's end is on the job it is owed nothing. The end is written on the runner's own floor, as for a Stop a runner carried while it held the job."
  - "Was true: a factory step's check receipts (`<transcript>.checks`, in codeaf's home) were not writable inside the fence, so every wrapped `go test`/`npm test` failed with `mktemp: … Operation not permitted` (exit 125) unless codeaf's home happened to sit under a folder the fence keeps writable (r2 passed only that way). Is true now: executor.FenceOptions.Receipts names that folder, the stage's fence makes it and lets the step write it (verification.ReceiptRoot), and nothing else of codeaf's home is writable."
  - "Was true: a step out of rounds on its checks asked `test is not green after 3 rounds: verification blocked: cd /Users/…/factory-demo-211 && … · try again…`, the raw shell lines in the Needs you row. Is true now: the question counts them, `test is not green after 3 rounds: 2 checks it ran were not confirmed to pass · …`; the commands stay in the step's record, its log line and its next round's brief."
  - "Was true: every open job manager checked ten times a second whether its job was stopped by listing and decoding every job document on the floor (seamRunDoor.managed), about 8.5 ms and 1.9 MB per check on a six-job floor; the third live run's engine idled at 145–188% of a core. Is true now: the answer is remembered with the file it came from and a check of an unchanged job decodes nothing (about 4.5 µs, 1.7 KB); a step's budget check does the same. PERF.md \"Idle factory manager checks\"."
  - "Was true: a Reject of an AgentField step's plan still stamped the step `tool_error` (\"the AgentField run cancelled: approval_rejected: …\") and logged `step_failed tool_error · build · stopped`, though the job ended Rejected; the desk's rail read failed. Is true now: the step ends as a native Reject leaves its step, failed with no failure on it or on the job and no step_failed event, keyed on the run's decision (factory.AgentFieldRejectedEnd), never on a step name."
