A long title pushed every number off the row, and on the row nothing read first:
the name, the bill, the meter and the state wore the same dot between them. Two
rows, each with one job — who and where on the seam, numbers and aliveness on the
row — and the numbers grouped by the question each answers.

Then the owner ran it. Five things came back, and they are all one thing: a row
that reads at a glance says what is TRUE OF NOW and says it once. So the rate at
the right edge is the stream's own figure rather than an average of something
that has finished; the `via` rider is on the seam whoever served, because a
rider that comes and goes reads as a lost sighting; what the cache saved is
derived from the reads rather than remembered from the turns, so it survives a
resume; and the two counts about somewhere else — the open conversations, the
standing orders — went to the two places that already draw those things, the tab
strip and the task column.
