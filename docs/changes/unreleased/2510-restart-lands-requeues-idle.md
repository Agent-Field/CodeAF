  - "Was true (#2508): a job re-taken after a restart while its AgentField step's run went on followed the run to its end, then reported a step named after one of the run's own calls (`execute`) and the factory refused the whole report with HTTP 422 \"report names an unknown step\"; the run's pull request opened but the job sat in Needs you (`results_not_saved`), jobs 72 and 74 of the fourth live run. The cause was the wire: the run's calls are folded under their step in `Job.Steps` for the rail, and `api.ToItem` read them back as phases of the job. Is true now: `unsteps` skips the rows folded from `Job.AgentFieldSteps`, so a re-taken runner's phases are the pipeline's own and the job lands on its run's pull request."
  - "Was true: every job taken back after a restart got an event `step_failed` reading \" ·  · \" at pickup, from the expiry of a hold that had stamped no failure. Is true now: expiry records step_failed only for a failure it stamped."
  - "Was true: a native job whose step was running when codeaf quit could lose its hold with no requeue: the quitting runner settled the step's last spending through the receipt door (`ReconcileReport`), which released the live hold and left the job running with nobody holding it, so expiry never saw it (Task A of the fourth live run, until Pause → Resume). #2508's follow only covered jobs with a live AgentField run (which spend nothing here, so they never reached that door). Is true now: a hold that door releases on a running job is requeued exactly as an expired hold: an interrupted step, retried automatically within its limit."
  - "Was true: an open step chat (a conversation that manages no job) remembered its stop check against the floor folder's own identity, which moved every few seconds — the runner registry was rewritten on every worker request, plus heartbeats and poll records — so each open step chat listed and decoded every job again; the fourth live run's engine idled at 150% of a core. Is true now: it reads only the job documents that changed, from one shared look at the floor's job files per 100 ms, and a worker's presence is saved to the registry at most every 15 s. PERF.md \"Idle factory manager checks\"."
  - "Was true: a runner restart added a full set of rows to the runner registry (15 became 30) and the replaced process's rows stayed for good, since only the next attach forgot quiet rows. Is true now: an idle pull forgets its own computer's rows once quiet past the runner timeout and holding no lease."
