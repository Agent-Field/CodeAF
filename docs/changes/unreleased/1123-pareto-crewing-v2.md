The picker's one dial is now a stake in dollars, and the thing it learns from is a
grade no model can move. `frugal` and `balanced` are $1 and $10 of stake read off the
cost-quality front; `max` is the best crew on it. The chat door grades every landing
with gofmt, build, vet and the touched packages' tests, and records that — not the
judge's opinion — as `acceptable` per seat under the source that graded it; the seed
carries the same metric from the `reviewer` source, the relay carries both metrics on
the wire, and the front is priced on what this install's own tasks spent per seat.
`docs/design/model-pool/` holds the paper, its figures and the text-free CSVs behind
them. Still design only: price bands, cascades, the capped exploration budget, the
switch guard, and a custom stake.
