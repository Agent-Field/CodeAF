The sizing judge and the division reviewer answer two different questions. The judge
reads the person's sentence and says whether the WORK is wide; the reviewer reads one
set of parts drawn out of that sentence, before anybody has opened the material, and
says whether THOSE parts are separate jobs. A no to the second is not a no to the
first — so the worker carries on as one worker, and keeps the verb for the parts it can
see once it has the material in front of it.
