Typing at home and pressing enter has always meant "start a new conversation and
send this". From the eighth conversation open in a window it meant something
else. Home closed itself, asked for a conversation, was refused by the keeper's
cap — and sent the sentence anyway, into whatever was in front. By then the
screen and the box were gone, so there was nowhere else for the words to go and
nowhere for the refusal to be read except inside a conversation nobody had asked
to be in.

The question the typed-path branch of that same row had been asking all along is
now asked by both of them, before anything is closed and in the same words. A
refusal costs a keystroke and leaves the sentence exactly where it was typed.
