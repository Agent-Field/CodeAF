---
kind: fixed
title: Long jobs can continue beyond a thirty dollar budget
pr: 0
surface: [engine]
invalidates:
  - "Spending thirty dollars left a job with only Stop and refused continuation."
---

Budget questions always offer a next cap. Above the standard $5, $10, $20 and
$30 choices, the cap doubles until it is above recorded spending. A job capped
at $30 with $31 spent offers $60; Yes and the raise button both set that cap.
Other nonstandard amounts remain refused.
