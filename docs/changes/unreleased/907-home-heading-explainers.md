The seven home panels each answer a question a person has when they walk up to the screen. The word alone said which panel, and the whisper said what arrives there once the panel was open — but in a tall frame a panel's heading was the only thing above a column of rows, and there was nothing between the word and the rows that said what this panel *is* for. A short explainer, in the dim ink, under the heading's own, named beside each one: `needs you · questions & checks`, `where you were · enter reopens one`, `projects · folders you've opened`, `running · work you sent off`, `since you left · while you were gone`, `next up · reminders & routines`. Spend, whose right-hand clause already says what the panel is for, carries none. The explainer is dropped whole where the width cannot hold it beside the heading.
