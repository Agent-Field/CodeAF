title: work only a person can do goes to the person, not to a worker

The reader that weighs a drawn division may find that what is left of the work
cannot be done by a worker at all — an approving review only a named person may
give, a credential nobody here holds, somebody else's system doing something by
itself. It said exactly that about a task whose whole remainder was two GitHub
approvals, before the worker had made a single request, and the finding was
journalled and thrown away: nine minutes and $1.24 across the run, the check, a
repair round and the check again, spent editing a file in an empty copy of the
repository, and failed by the check at the end.

The reader now has a word for it, and it is a field it has to reach for rather
than a phrase anybody greps: a cautious refusal must never park doable work on
somebody. Where it comes back, the task lands needing your look before a worker
is started, with that reader's own sentence as its report.
