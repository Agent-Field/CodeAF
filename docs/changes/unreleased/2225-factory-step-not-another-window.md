After Take over and Give back, a job step the factory itself is running no longer posts that it is open in another codeaf window, and the step's row does not say it can't be opened. When that step's chat is free again it opens without an "open here again" note. A crew member that really is open in another window still shows the same notice as before. A job step open in a window this app cannot reach is not announced either; its questions still go through the factory.
