Lands after #462, which made every other shape's page name exactly the tools its
call carries. A hand was the shape that law could not reach by rendering: it
opens on the caller's transcript and the caller's page word for word, and that
shared prefix is the whole economy of forking, so a page composed for the hand
would end the sharing at byte one.

Appending keeps both. The page stays where it was, and the note under it says
whose page that is and what this reader actually has. A model that reaches past
the list is answered `Unknown tool` as it always was — what changed is that it
is no longer being told, two paragraphs earlier, that the verb is there.
