---

What was false: the door home was built at the bottom of the conversation
router, where the only page was the conversation itself. When the places came
and each got a box of its own, the gesture was still a conversation's gesture —
a person on the tasks filter who cleared it and typed two spaces got two
spaces in the filter, and the manual said that was the design.

What is true now: the door sits at the bottom of the place router, under every
other meaning a space could have — the whole-keyboard layers a place claims
for itself, the router's own six classes, and the place's own reading of the
key — and the only thing that reaches it is a plain space falling toward the
place's box. Two of those spaces, in a box that has no other words, open home
from every place that has a door at all.

The review of this change found the door's own hole: memory's card editor is
a whole-keyboard layer that lived below it, inside the place's own key
handler, and its box is the box the door reads — so the second space on a
nearly-cleared card armed the door and stood the person on home mid-edit.
`placeMemory.owns` now holds the keyboard while the card editor is open, the
same claim the settings panel makes for its value editor, and a test drives
the editor from a real memory line and holds the two spaces against the door.
