The note on the panel was that the difference between `needs you` and `to check` is not
obvious, and the fix is one dim clause on the group's line rather than a label on every row.
Seven words, once, in the place a heading's clause already lives — and the cursor row's
`1 accept   2 not right` says what checking one actually means.

Two things the tmux drive found that unit tests had not: the same landing drawn on two rows
of one panel whenever its conversation is live, and that `a`/`n` cannot be answer keys on a
screen whose foot promises that typing starts a conversation.
