The owner's reading of it was "the right side preview — make the spacing a bit more
wide… it seems small" (#212). The spacing was the smaller half of the fault: one
blank row between every band is a rhythm that carries no information, so the address
under the title, the bill under the work and the verbs under the bill all stood the
same distance apart and a card of seven bands read as seven facts in a heap. The gap
says which group a band is in now, and the section words step one role up so the eye
has something to navigate by between the title and the dim floor.
