invalidates:
  - "The end-of-turn reader was asked whether the ask was finished over a task that had already landed done with its own checks passing, said no, and the reply was carried on three times and then told the person the work was unfinished. The live-task gate already ends the reply that handed the work out; the landing's half now ends the reply that reports it done, so a green settled card is not re-asked as unfinished."
  - "A woken reply was always read for what remains, whatever it cost, with only a question to the person or a broken reply stopping the reader. A landing that is this request, done, with its checks green, now stops the reader too. A piece of a larger ask, a landing nobody checked, and an incomplete landing are still read."
---

The reader looks at a short account of what was said, not at the tree or the
checks that already ran. Believing that line over a settled green card is how a
finished ask was carried on three times and then reported unfinished. The gate
is the graph's own fact, the same family as handing the work out while it is
still live.
