The app command opens a scoped editing turn that works on definitions. The
desktop can draw screens on synthetic records and return layout findings.
Check exercises automations with fake connectors. Apply publishes the
checked version when the person is ready.

The Builder reads one relevant kit descriptor at a time and keeps definition
and Check replies compact. Failed jobs record what stopped the app and show
the reason in build progress.

With Desk connected, Check measures layouts in both themes at two widths
and layout findings block Apply. CLI-only builds record that Look was skipped
and can publish after Check. Collection recipes order typed columns, provide
empty states, and add views and summary metrics from record fields.

A connected Desk capture failure asks you to retry Check. A stale Apply
leaves the draft available to discard, and opening an unused app shelf
still lets the desktop join another computer.
