---
Remote control is off until it is turned on, on both computers: `/settings` → `Devices` → `remote control`, or `CODEAF_REMOTE_CONTROL=1`. Turning it on or off in a running terminal takes effect at once, with no restart. Turning it off refuses new live views at once and ends open ones within about 30 seconds with `<name> turned remote control off.`, after which the viewer does not redial by itself; the chats keep running, a live view's folder never becomes Home's project, and a quit leaves a live view of another computer's chat out of what it records for resume.
Live traffic is sealed end to end, so the relay cannot read it and stores none of it; with relay storage off the relay keeps no words, titles or folder names of your chats, only the small directory record that says which computer holds each chat and whether a turn is running. The relay sees how much traffic there is, when, and which of your computers are connected.
Limits: one line is at most 256 KiB; a live view is paced at 1 MiB/s each way; the host keeps a soft allowance of 256 MiB of relay traffic per UTC day (`CODEAF_REMOTE_DAILY_MB`; spent, views turn watch only until 00:00 UTC, and direct traffic is not counted); live views take 14 of a computer's 16 relay streams, two stay free for Move; a live stream above 4 MiB/s for 5 s is cut.
One computer types into a live chat at a time; the other reads `typing from <name> now · enter types here`, its first `enter` takes the keyboard and sends nothing, and letters or commands typed while it does not hold the keyboard go nowhere.
The direct road needs no setup and falls back to the relay. It links the pion WebRTC stack, which grows the binary by about 9.5 MB; with remote control off, or with `CODEAF_REMOTE_ROAD=relay`, none of it runs.
