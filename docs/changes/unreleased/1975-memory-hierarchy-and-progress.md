Memory uses clear heading, text and numeric colors, hover feedback on switches
and controls, and progress that wraps within the article sidebar. Stage counts
are measured items rather than elapsed-time or overall-completion estimates.

The first build drains a fixed admitted history boundary before organization.
Catalog-aware routing reuses existing page identities. The initial concept tree
receives a whole-collection review before independent guides are written.
Short titles and two-level folders follow the actual subjects rather than fixed
project categories. Topic maps stay with their pages.

Claims retain original citations and assistant reports stay attributed. Changed
and retired evidence refreshes affected guides; exact quote spans prevent
unrelated guidance from being invalidated. Human prose and deleted concepts
remain protected. New background evidence flags edited pages for review.

Incomplete provider results and invalid organization references preserve pending
work. Failed plans are not cached as valid results. Generated prose must pass
both evidence support and lasting-usefulness checks. Existing published page
identities are retained; this does not automatically merge older published
concepts or rewrite human edits. Oversized concepts retain an explicit state
rather than silently truncating their evidence.
