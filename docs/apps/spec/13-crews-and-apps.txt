6 · Rules
Final behaviour for links between crews and apps.
Link, never copy Items hold links to records, documents and jobs. Nothing is duplicated into the crew; edits always go to the app.
Who can see A linked item shows to crew viewers only if they can open it in its app; otherwise the row reads "CRM record · no access".
Record deleted The row stays, dimmed: "Deleted in CRM". Archived app: "CRM is archived · Restore" (Owner/Admin).
Unlink Right-click a row → Unlink. The record and its "Discussed in" history stay.
Finish crew Stops members, the app's agent leaves the crew, links stay on the records as "Discussed in: Pricing push · finished".
Approvals Approvals raised by an app's agent go to the person who asked the crew for the work (here you), never to the manager.

1 A crew is a crew. Pricing push (sage) is an ordinary crew: a manager (@pricing), members, the window frame tinted sage. Its job here is unstructured work: talking through prices, deciding, writing. Apps hold the structured records; the crew links them.
2 An app's agent can be a crew member. The CRM agent shows its badge on every turn: from CRM · can read deals, draft emails . The badge lists the CRM app's capabilities that it has; nothing else. One live copy per crew, like any member; it spends from CRM's budget, not the crew's.
3 The manager asks, the app's agent does. @pricing → CRM agent is a normal member-to-member message (also in Log). The CRM agent creates and changes records through CRM , under CRM's rules and record history; each change is listed with the app version it ran on.
4 Normal approvals. Sending email leaves the workspace, so each draft waits: Approval needed: send email , here, in Inbox and on the CRM rail row. The same approval card everywhere; answer it once. Approve sends as you with a 5-second Undo.
5 Items shows what the crew links: CRM records (deal rows), a Slides deck, files the crew made and factory jobs. A click opens the record or deck in its own app tab (CRM, Slides), never inside the crew. Records show their current state, read live from the app.
6 Link a record, document or job opens ⌘K filtered to apps' records and documents plus factory jobs. Linking never copies: remove the link and the record is untouched.
7 Crew ⌄ : Rename… · Change tint · Add a member… (agents and apps' agents) · Replace manager… · Finish crew… . Finish crew is enabled once nothing waits on you.
