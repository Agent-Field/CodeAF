Spaces, the parts of an app, its two agents, roles and lifecycles, drawn. Words follow the S-A0 glossary.
1 · Spaces Personal · Workspace · one person in several
A space is one codeaf server and everything on it. Personal is the server inside your own desktop app. A workspace is the same codeaf binary, run by a company. You can belong to several; the switcher at the top of the rail picks one.

2 · Anatomy of an app files → kit + bridge → shell → server → runners and connectors
An app is files. CodeAF renders the screens from them with kit parts; every action goes through the bridge to the space's server, which checks it against app.json and the caller's role before it touches records, runs or connectors.

4 · Roles workspace roles × actions · app roles × actions · signatories
Two independent levels. A workspace role says what you may do in the space; an app role says what you may do in one app. Dot = allowed, dash = not allowed.

Signatories
Named roles an app declares in app.json for SignOff chains.
Examples: “Quality lead” , “Second checker” . An Editor assigns people in App ⌄ → Members.
A chain asks each signatory in order; each sees it in the Inbox as “Approval needed: sign-off” .
The person who made a record can never sign it.
A signatory needs at least User on the app. Being one grants nothing else.
Defaults
Whoever makes an app is its Editor . A new workspace app gives every other member User ; Editors change it in Members.
Owner and Admin are not Editors by role: to Shape they need Editor like anyone.
A workspace has exactly one Owner; the Owner can hand it to an Admin.
In Personal you are Owner and Editor of everything; no roles are shown.

Check fails 3 rounds. Building stops and shows the error: Fix it (one more round with your words) · Cancel (back to Planning).
Discard in Preview. Nothing is kept: no app, no records. The tab closes.
A change breaks the app. The card keeps the last working version, dimmed: “This change broke CRM · Fix it · Revert” .

In Personal Apply makes the next version for you alone. Move to workspace… brings files and records into a workspace as that app's next version.
