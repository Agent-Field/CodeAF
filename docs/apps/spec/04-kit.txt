"New app…" opens the New app sheet. Each app adds its own commands as App: Command ; they show in ⌘K for everyone who sees the app.
1 Apps is the fourth rail section, after Crews and Agents. The rail shows the space picked in the switcher at the top (acme ⌄; Personal · acme · globex). Inside acme every row is a workspace app: acme (the factory) first, then the others (CRM, Support, Hiring) by name; drag to reorder. Inbox shows the current space only; an amber dot on the switcher means another space needs you. Archived apps sit in a collapsed Archived group at the bottom of the section. Each row is a 16px glyph with no tint square (squares mean crews). The right edge shows one state glyph: amber needs you, accent running, nothing when idle. Click opens the app in a tab, or brings its tab forward.
2 No personal rows in acme. Personal apps live in your Personal space — switch with the space switcher at the top of the rail. Workspace apps carry no glyph; the right edge shows only the state glyph.
3 New app… is always the last app row, above the collapsed Archived group, quiet (--ink-3, plus icon), no state glyph. Click, or ⌘K → "New app…" , opens this sheet over the current card. The sheet hangs from the top of the card, 600px wide; Esc or Cancel closes it and nothing is created.
4 Factory is CodeAF’s prebuilt app. acme opens like every app: CodeAF's header line (glyph, name, "CodeAF’s prebuilt app · v3 · shaped by @santosh", Shape · Agent · App ⌄), then the app's own content. Its icon is live (animated) while jobs run.
5 Only the card content is generated. Everything below the header line (here Jobs · Repos · …, the query field, the job list) is the app's code, built from the CodeAF kit and tokens. Window, rail, tab strip, header line, Inbox, ⌘K, menus and shortcuts are CodeAF's and look the same in every app.
6 Create app ↵ (⌘↵ from the field) is enabled once the description has 12 or more words. It opens a new tab "CRM · building", adds the row to the rail and starts the Builder. Home defaults to the space open in the switcher (here acme); in the Personal space it is Personal. Budget defaults to "up to $20 a month"; connectors list only accounts already connected in Settings → Accounts.
