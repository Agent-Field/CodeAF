Seven apps from seven kinds of team, each drawn as its app would render: the CodeAF window and app header, then the app's main screen in real HTML on CodeAF tokens. Under each, every kit part the screen uses, by tier, and the kit v2 part, feature or rule that covers each further need, or Backlog. On screen, a green outline marks a kit v2 part and a grey outline a backlog item. Shape mode, the plan card and the draft bar build every app, so they are not listed per app.
