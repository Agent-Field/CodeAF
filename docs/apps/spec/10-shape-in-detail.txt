1 Use · Shape. Shape is enabled for Editors only. Switching on keeps your place: the screen you were on opens in the centre, the pane becomes the Builder · shaping the app , and the draft bar appears. Switching back to Use keeps the draft for later (it survives restarts).
2 Draft bar : Draft · 2 changes · the quoted requests · Show diff · Apply · Discard · Versions. Apply opens the Apply sheet (migration preview when a field changes; card below); its sub-line says “everyone in acme gets v13”. Discard drops every change after one confirm.
3 The plan as a menu : Data · Automation · Screens · Agent · Needs. Click a screen to run it in the centre; click a record type or run to see it in plain words and edit a line in place (that edit becomes a change in the draft). A line the draft changes shows new .
4 Your draft, running. The centre is the real Pipeline with the draft applied, on real records. reads real data · saves to a scratch copy : moving a deal here writes to a scratch overlay only you see; connectors run as fakes, so nothing is sent.
5 Each request is one change , stored as the request text plus its diff. Every change runs Check (schema · kit law · render every screen · behaviour on connector fakes) before it joins the draft. A failing check goes back to the Builder, max 3 rounds, then shows to you.
6 Teammates keep using v12 while your draft is open. Several people may have drafts at once; whoever applies first wins and the others get their requests replayed (cards below).
