Reconnect
Reconnect opens Google sign-in for mona@acme.com; when it returns, the step runs at once and the Inbox line moves to Done today. The rail row of CRM shows the amber dot meanwhile.
1 The consent sheet opens the first time a workspace app acts as you through one of your personal connections: here @mona approved an email and CRM needs her Gmail. It is asked once per person, per app, per connection. The title always names the app, the action and the person: "CRM may send email as Mona".
2 CRM can / can't is written from the app's Needs (app.json capabilities), never by the app's agent. Sending still goes through "Approval needed: send email" every time; Allow only lets CRM use the account.
3 Allow saves the permission for Mona + CRM + Gmail and the approved email sends at once (5-second Undo). Not now keeps the email waiting; Mona's Inbox gets "CRM wants to send email as you · Allow". Other people's use of CRM is not affected.
4 Revoke anytime in Settings → Accounts → Apps that act as you (see 6). After a revoke the sheet shows again the next time CRM needs it.
5 Same sheet for every personal connection (Calendar, Slack, GitHub). In Personal there is no sheet: Personal apps use your accounts directly, because only you are there.
6 Settings → Accounts (the app's own Settings, ⌘,) lists your personal connections, the same in every space: status Connected or Expired · Reconnect (red glyph, Reconnect is the primary button). Disconnect asks first and lists the apps that will stop acting as you. Apps that act as you lists every Allow, with its space; Revoke takes effect at once and the app asks again next time.
7 Workspace settings → Connections holds the accounts that belong to acme (support@, Stripe, the GitHub org). Members see the list; only Admins and Owners add, change or remove. Add connection… signs in to the service as the workspace and asks who can use it: All apps or Chosen apps . Apps use a workspace connection with no per-person sheet; sending still needs approval.
8 Needs shows every connection the app uses and as whom : "as each person" (a personal connection, each person allows once) or "acme workspace" (a workspace connection). Your own status sits on the right with an Allow button; Editors also see "3 of 4 allowed". A run that needs a person who hasn't allowed waits in that person's Inbox.
9 Fakes. Every connector has a fake. Preview (when making an app), Shape drafts and Check always run against fakes: the bar says "Gmail is a fake in this draft · nothing is sent", and what would have happened is listed with a striped fake tag. Real connections are used only after Apply, by the applied version.
10 Removing a workspace connection (Admin, ⋯ → Remove…) always shows this dialog listing every app that uses it, with what for. Remove signs CodeAF out of the service, writes one Activity line "Mona removed Stripe · used by 2 apps", and those apps' Needs show "Stripe · ask an Admin". With no apps using it, the dialog is a plain confirm.
11 An expired connection never fails a run silently. The step that needs it waits (amber) and the connection's owner gets one Inbox line: "CRM can't send: Mona's Gmail expired · Reconnect". A personal connection → that person; a workspace connection → every Admin. After 7 days of waiting the run stops and moves to Done with "stopped · Gmail expired". Other people's runs keep going.
