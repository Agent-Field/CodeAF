1 The space switcher is the first row of the rail, above Inbox. It shows the current space: its square, its name and "Workspace" (or "only you" for Personal). Click it or press ⌃⌘0 to open this menu; ⌃⌘1–⌃⌘9 jump straight to a space in menu order. The amber dot means another space has something waiting for you.
2 Personal is always first and always there. It is the codeaf server inside your own app on this Mac: only you, your own accounts, your Personal apps. It cannot be left or deleted.
3 Workspaces follow in the order you joined them. Each row: square, name, server address, your workspace role, and how many apps you can open there. The current one has a check. Picking one swaps the whole rail and the tab strip to that space; the tabs of the space you left are kept and come back when you return.
4 A space that needs you shows an amber dot after its name. Inbox always shows the current space only; the dot on the switcher is how you learn about the others.
5 Join a workspace… opens a field for an invite link or a server address (see 9). New workspace… asks for a name and the address of a codeaf server your team runs ( codeaf serve , the same binary); you become its Owner.
6 Workspace settings… opens the settings window of the current workspace: General · Members · Connections · Spending · Apps. Members see it read only; Owner and Admins change it. In Personal this row reads Settings… and opens the app's own Settings (⌘,).
7 The header says where an app lives : "Workspace app · acme", the version, who shaped it last and your app role (Editor · User · Viewer). Runs of a workspace app run on the acme server, never on this Mac.
8 One rail per space. Switching space swaps every section (Crews, Agents, Apps) and the Inbox. Nothing from one space shows in another: a Personal app never appears in a workspace rail and a workspace app never appears in Personal. To move an app use App ⌄ → Move to workspace… (records move with it).
9 Joining a workspace. The link ( codeaf://join/acme.codeaf.dev/… ) opens CodeAF at step 1; pasting it into Join a workspace… does the same. Step 2 uses whatever sign-in the workspace server is set to (Google, GitHub or email code). Step 3 states the role the inviter chose and the app role each app gives "Everyone in acme". Open acme switches to it. A used or expired link shows "This invite has expired · Ask @mona for a new one".
10 Workspace roles. Owner pays for and can delete the workspace; Admin invites, removes Members, adds workspace connections and sets budgets; Member uses and makes apps. Everyone sees this list; only Owner and Admins see the controls. Only an Owner can make or unmake an Owner. The role chip opens the menu shown on @raj.
11 Remove from acme… asks first ("@raj loses access to acme now. Apps he made stay; records he made stay; his personal connections stop working in acme apps."). Invites expire after 7 days. The last Owner can't leave : Leave is disabled with the reason underneath, and the last Owner can't be made Admin or Member.
12 App roles are per app: Editor · User · Viewer. "Everyone in acme" is the first row and sets the default (User when an app is first Applied). A person row overrides it; a grey role means it comes from Everyone. The maker of an app is its first Editor. An app always keeps at least one Editor; Owners and Admins can open Members on any app to fix that.
13 A User clicks Shape (or the CRM agent's Shape this button): Shape stays dimmed and this popover opens. It names the Editor who shaped the app last (@mona). Ask @mona puts a line in her Inbox with the request; Raj gets "Mona shaped CRM · v13" in Activity when it lands, or "Mona said not now".
14 A Viewer (@jm on CRM) sees every screen with no Use · Shape switch, a "Viewer · read only" tag, create buttons disabled, fields as plain text and no drag. The CRM agent answers a Viewer's questions and never changes records for them. Viewers can't comment or approve.
15 Sign-off roles. The Builder names the roles from the plan (here Maker · Checker · Quality lead for a SignOff chain); Editors rename them, change the order and assign people (only people with app role User or Editor). The rule "maker can never check own work" is fixed. When a step would have nobody eligible, the sheet warns now, and a stuck record goes to the Editors' Inbox as "Refund #2041 has no Quality lead who didn't make it".
