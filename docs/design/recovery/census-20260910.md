## 8. Findings

1. **The 60 s/90 s stream wall is the single biggest error class and it is ours.**
   869 `decode stream: context deadline exceeded`, `ms` at exactly 90,000 (427)
   and 60,000 (210); 780 had already received a first token. 413 are one lane,
   OpenInference on deepseek-v4-flash-0731.
2. **`deadline_ms` is fiction.** 2,720 of 11,841 finishes ran more than 2× their
   `deadline_ms`; the field is `10000` on 7,937 rows. Worst: `deadline_ms 10000`,
   `ms 937777`. `silence_ms` equals `deadline_ms` exactly on 3,014 of 5,238 rows.
3. **Retries hammer the same lane.** 53 % of multi-attempt chains never switch;
   three chains burned 16–17 consecutive attempts on one lane over ~11 minutes and
   still ended 429. `retry_after` is never recorded.
4. **`lane` and the serving provider disagree constantly.** 3,728 of 10,107
   finishes have `served != lane`; on 358 of 1,103 errors naming a provider the
   `(via X)` contradicts `lane` — `lane: Fireworks` / `via DeepInfra` 104×,
   `lane: DeepInfra` / `via Morph` 31×, `lane: Baidu` / `via OpenInference` 26×.
5. **Health is getting worse.** Clean-200 rate by day: 90.6, 80.5, 90.4, 73.9,
   83.3, 77.7, 76.3, **54.6, 39.0**, 69.8, **61.4**. 2026-09-08: 301 429s in
   1,018 calls.
6. **Failed calls record no cost and no tokens.** 1,884 of 1,887 in-stream
   failures have `completion_tokens` absent and `cost` 0. Total recorded spend
   $201.15; $0.00 attributed to failures. Hedging waste measured at $8.91.
7. **Hedging is the error factory.** 2,343 hedged finishes (767 two-arm, 1,576
   three-arm); 513 (22 %) end 200-clean. The losing arms are most of the 1,204
   `context canceled` rows.
8. **`cost_s` is numerically broken.** Median 9.29, p99 3.3e+45, max 1.99e+146.
   2,221 rows note `cost_s was +Inf and is not on this row`; 12 rows say `the
   belief file could not be compacted: json: unsupported value: NaN`.
9. **527 attempts have a start row and no finish row** (3.1 %). 2,830 finishes
   carry no `tag` — mostly `deepseek-v4-flash-0731` (1,723).
10. `API error (400): … Could not decrypt the provided encrypted_content` — 8
    rows in 65 s on 2026-09-09, `x-ai/grok-4.20-multi-agent`, 7 labelled
    `lane: Friendli`. `API error (400): glm is not a valid model ID` once — a bare
    alias reached the wire.
