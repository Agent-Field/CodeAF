\section{How codeaf runs it}
\label{app:engineering}

The mechanism as the code stands, with the package, type and function that does
each step named and every absent capability said plainly.

\subsection{System overview}
\label{app:overview}

The seat is picked by \code{internal/crewpick} off the Pareto front of expected
bill against crew quality. \code{wirePoolIndex} in \code{cmd/codeaf/poolindex.go}
seats its three inputs at start-up: \code{config.AutoIndex}, the measurement
document, \code{config.AutoOwnCells}, the install's own judged scores, and
\code{config.AutoShapes}, what real tasks spent on each seat.

A chat landing reaches \code{poolJudgeHook} in \code{cmd/codeaf/poolrecord.go},
which grades the workspace with \code{grade.Grade} in \code{internal/pool/grade}
and no model in it, asks \code{judge.Judge} in \code{internal/pool/judge},
observes both readings with \code{record.Recorder} in \code{internal/pool/record},
and appends one row per scored seat to \code{outbox.Outbox} in
\code{internal/pool/outbox}. \code{poolPush} in
\code{cmd/codeaf/poolinstall.go} posts the rows to the relay, where
\code{relay/src/worker.js} validates them against \code{relay/src/schema.js} and
folds each install into its own running total in KV through
\code{relay/src/sheet.js}, before signing the document it publishes in
\code{relay/src/index.js}. An install pulls and parses it (\code{internal/pool/pull},
\code{internal/pool/index}) for its next pick, and a workflow mirrors it to an
orphan branch.

\subsection{Seat resolution}
\label{app:seats}

\begingroup\emergencystretch=2em
The profile's pick word picks one of three routes, \code{CrewPickTable} (the
default), \code{CrewPickCatalog} and \code{CrewPickLearn} in
\code{internal/config/crew.go}, all resolved through one seam in
\code{internal/config/auto.go}: \code{pickedSeat} for a seat the pick answers
for, \code{autoRow} for a bare row that says auto.
\par\endgroup

The computation is shared. \code{autoCandidates} reads each catalog row as a
\code{crewpick.Candidate}, dropping an unpublished price and scaling the tariff
to dollars per million with \code{perMillion}, and \code{AutoShapes} supplies the
seat shapes, or \code{crewpick.DefaultShapes}. \code{crewpick.FrontWith} keeps
the crews no other crew beats on both bill and quality;
\code{crewpick.Presets} reads \code{frugal} at \code{StakeFrugal}, a dollar, and
\code{balanced} at \code{StakeBalanced}, ten dollars, off that front, and
\code{max} as its last crew; \code{AtStake} is the argmin of \code{Loss}, which
is \code{TaskBill} plus the stake times one minus quality; and
\code{crewpick.ShapesFrom} learns each seat's volume share and input-to-output
ratio from \code{shape.Tasks} over the usage ledger, shrunk at
\code{ShapeWeightAt}.

\code{autoPrior} blends the evidence: the index's cells of
\code{PoolQualityMetric} (\code{acceptable}, a Bernoulli metric) through
\code{crewpick.PriorFromCells} at the document's \code{min\_installs}, the
install's own cells through a second \code{PriorFromCells} at a floor of one,
\code{crewpick.MergePriors} folding the two by observation count, and
\code{crewpick.SeatQualityWith} moving a seat's catalog quality towards a rating
by \code{N/(N+PriorWeightAt)}. With no index, \code{autoIndex} answers nil and
\code{MergePriors} returns the own prior alone, so the install still learns from
its own graded runs; with neither source the prior is nil and every seat keeps
its catalog quality, \code{poolIndexFor} falling back to the build's embedded
seed through \code{index.Fallback}. \code{ownSheetSummary} in
\code{cmd/codeaf/pool.go}, printed by \code{printPool}, counts the same
\code{own.json} that \code{poolOwnCellsFor} hands the picker.

\code{poolGradeLanding} grades first and consults no model: \code{grade.Grade}
runs four stages in order, stopping at the first failure, formatting over the
changed Go files, \code{go build ./...} over the module, \code{go vet} over the
touched packages, and \code{go test -count=1} over those same packages with the
cache defeated. The source is \code{codeaf/grader} when the tests ran and
passed and \code{codeaf/grader-build} when they did not finish in budget or there
were none. The package states the absence law: no \code{go.mod} at the workspace
root answers not-ok and nothing is recorded, because a capability that cannot
work is absent rather than broken. \code{grade-last.json} holds the moment, the task id,
the source word, the pass bit, the failing stage and its detail, the touched
packages and the seconds.

\code{poolAskJudges} asks the judges. \code{judge.Candidates} lists the priced
catalog rows that can judge the crew, none of them a seat the crew already holds,
and \code{judge.Judge} puts at most three of them in turn to one question per
seat the landing held, reading each answer as one JSON object with a 0-100 score
and letting a failing seat fail alone. \code{judge-last.json} holds the
moment, the task id, and either the judge that answered with the seats it scored
or the candidates tried and the last error's line.

\code{record.Recorder} records. \code{RecordGrade} observes one
\code{acceptable} cell per seat the crew held, 100 when the grade passed and 0
otherwise, and appends one row where the judge column names the grader;
\code{Record} observes each judge score under \code{role\_quality} and appends
one row per score, and \code{record.SaveSheet} then writes the sheet.
\code{markJudged} writes one empty marker per run id and attempt under
\code{judged}, so the sweep never judges a run twice, a sheet that would not load
or save being the one outcome left unmarked. The picker's own-cells seam is
repointed at the new cells and \code{poolPush} sends the outbox.

\code{poolJudgeSweep} runs at chat start over the headless doors'
\code{pending.jsonl} rows and the final-state landings a process death left
unjudged, skipping a run whose marker is there. Every sweep call site passes an
empty workspace, \code{poolJudgeSweep} for a resumed landing and
\code{sweepClaim} for a row it claims, because the tree the run changed may be
gone by then: the grade is skipped, only the judge reads, and the run is recorded
with a judge score and no grade.

\subsection{The relay}
\label{app:relay}

A row is one NDJSON line per scored seat: an envelope with a schema number, the
day and a nonce, and inside it the seat's score, which \code{relay/src/schema.js}
refuses unless every field matches. \code{cellKey} in \code{relay/src/sheet.js} renders the address a triple
lives at: a \code{role\_quality} row takes the five-segment form every stored
triple was written under, role, model, judge, door and size, while any other
metric leads with its own name, so an \code{acceptable} row's key is six segments
with the metric first. A stored value is
a triple of count, sum and sum of squares, and the relay adds the new score to
the triple already at that key.

\code{handleRows} in \code{relay/src/worker.js} keeps only the rows the relay has
not stored: a nonce repeated inside one batch is collapsed by a set, and a nonce
already stored under its per-install seen key is skipped, so a replayed outbox is
not folded twice; the seen key carries a time to live of one week.

The publication floor is \code{min\_installs}, three by default, and the relay
withholds no thin cell: it sorts the cells that meet the floor ahead of the ones
that do not. \code{internal/pool/index/index.go}
enforces the floor where the document is read, dropping a cell below it on the
\code{installs} field or on the row count when it carries none, and
\code{internal/crewpick} floors again on row counts for cells that never carried
an install count, on purpose, since rows cannot be fewer than the installs behind
them.

A cron trigger in \code{wrangler.toml} fires hourly, and a read republishes in
the background when the stored version is missing or older than
\code{PUBLISH\_EVERY}, one hour, answering what is stored so no caller waits.
\code{buildIndex} writes a fixed key order, so the same cells always render the
same bytes, and \code{signIndex} signs those exact bytes with Ed25519 as a
detached base64 signature.

The write path takes no maximum: the worker folds each install's rows into that
install's own running total, and \code{relay/src/sheet.js}'s \code{join}, the
componentwise maximum of two triples, is defined and called only by its own test. The GitHub mirror is a
workflow: it fetches the document and its signature, verifies with
\code{relay/verify.mjs} against the public key copied from \code{wrangler.toml},
and commits both to the orphan branch \code{model-pool} under \code{pool/}.

\subsection{How an install learns and contributes}
\label{app:install}

\begingroup\emergencystretch=2em
One install writes, under the profile's pool directory:
\code{own.json}, the tally sheet \code{record.SaveSheet} writes;
\code{outbox.jsonl}, the rows waiting to leave, appended by
\code{outbox.Append}; \code{install}, the 32-character nonce that is the
install's whole identity at the relay; \code{doc.json} with its signature, the
puller's cache; \code{grade-last.json} and \code{judge-last.json}; and
\code{pending.jsonl} and the judged markers. Elsewhere on the machine,
\code{session.RecordUsage} appends every provider call to \code{usage.jsonl},
which \code{poolShapesFor} reads once at start-up through \code{shape.Tasks} and
\code{crewpick.ShapesFrom}, so a seat is priced on what real tasks spent on it.
\par\endgroup

What leaves the machine is the outbox, and only when the pool's mode allows
sending: \code{poolPush} posts the pending rows to the relay with the install
nonce in the \code{X-Codeaf-Install} header, at start-up and again after every
judged landing. The start-up refresh pulls the signed index on its own goroutine,
so a run's picks cannot move under it, and falls through to the mirror when the
relay does not answer.

What never leaves is the work. A row carries a score, a model id, a seat, a
door, a size class and a day, and no brief, report or path. The brief, the
deliverable, the report and the list of files written do travel, in the judge's
own prompt, to the model picked as judge, which is a call the machine makes to a
provider under the person's own credentials and bills to the judge's seat; none
of it is sent to the relay.

A fresh install has nothing under the pool directory, so no sheet, no outbox and
no nonce yet, the nonce being drawn on the first push. \code{shape.Tasks} finds
no seat rows, so the front is priced on the default shapes, the fixed shape of
the paper's baseline, and the empty own sheet leaves the prior the index's alone.
The index is the build's embedded seed, chosen by \code{index.Fallback} when no
cached document parses, so a first run picks from real numbers, and until its
first landing the install contributes nothing.
